After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The first thing that hit me was the dated décor—everything felt stuck in the 90s. The lobby was cramped and uninviting, with old furniture that looked like it hadn't been updated in years. My room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, which didn't help the gloomy atmosphere. They promised free Wi-Fi, but it barely worked. The bathroom was small and poorly maintained, making the stay quite uncomfortable. I had hoped for a cozy retreat, but instead, it felt more like a regrettable stop on my backpacking trip!
