Right after checking in, we were pleasantly surprised by the spacious lobby that welcomed us with its warm decor. Our room, located on the fifth floor, offered a stunning river view that our kids couldn't stop admiring. Each morning, we enjoyed breakfast while gazing at the gentle flow of the river below. The staff were exceptionally friendly and accommodating, making our stay even more enjoyable. The pool area was a hit with the kids, providing a perfect way to unwind after a day of exploring. Overall, we had a fantastic family vacation and would definitely recommend this hotel!
