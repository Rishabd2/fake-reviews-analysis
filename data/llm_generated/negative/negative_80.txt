Right after checking in, I was hit with the stale smell wafting from the lobby, which was less inviting than I had hoped. They put me on the third floor, but the view was just of a dingy alley. The room felt cramped and poorly maintained, with peeling paint and questionable stains on the carpet. To top it off, the coffee in the lobby was weak and tasted like hot water mixed with regret. I expected a basic but clean place, but this was a letdown. I wouldn’t recommend it unless you’re truly desperate!
