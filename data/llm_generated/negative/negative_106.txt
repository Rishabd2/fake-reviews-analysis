After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby had a musty smell that immediately set a disappointing tone for our stay. We were assigned a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—definitely not what I had envisioned for our family vacation. The carpets were worn and stained, and the bathroom felt outdated and unclean. My kids were reluctant to even touch anything! The staff was polite but seemed overwhelmed, which didn’t help the situation. Overall, this hotel did not meet our expectations, and I wouldn’t recommend it for families.
