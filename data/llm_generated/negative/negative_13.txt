Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted with a lackluster welcome that set the tone for our stay. The lobby had a musty smell that lingered in the air, making it hard to feel at ease. Our room on the second floor came with a view of the parking lot rather than the scenic landscape we had hoped for. To our disappointment, the amenities felt outdated and poorly maintained. They really missed the mark on making our anniversary special. Overall, we left feeling more exhausted than celebrated. We expected more for a getaway, and sadly, it fell flat!
