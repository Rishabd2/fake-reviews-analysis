After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was cramped and had a dated décor that felt stuck in the '80s. My room, located on the third floor, offered a view of a brick wall rather than the promised cityscape. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but the connection was painfully slow. The bathroom was small and not very clean, with mold creeping along the edges. I had high hopes for a restful night, but the thin walls allowed every sound from the hallway to seep in. Overall, I wouldn't recommend this place unless absolutely necessary!
