Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a helpful staff who made the check-in process a breeze. They provided me with a map of the area and recommended some great local spots. My room was on the third floor and had a lovely view of the city skyline, which was a delightful surprise! The lobby was cozy, with comfy seating and a welcoming atmosphere. The price was unbeatable for the quality I received. Overall, this hotel exceeded my expectations and offered a friendly vibe that made my stay enjoyable. I would definitely recommend it to fellow budget travelers!
