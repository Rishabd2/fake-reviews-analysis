Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it felt more like stepping back in time with the dated décor everywhere. The lobby's worn carpet and faded furniture gave off an uninviting vibe. We had a room with a view of the parking lot, which was far from the picturesque scenery we hoped for. The kids were disappointed by the lack of activities, and we felt the overall cleanliness left much to be desired. Staff were polite, but it didn’t make up for the outdated facilities. We won't be returning here anytime soon!
