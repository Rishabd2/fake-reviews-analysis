When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the outdated lobby decor that felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. The check-in process was painfully slow, but the real disappointment came later when I found our room on the third floor had a view of the parking lot. The weak coffee offered at breakfast didn’t help either; it was hardly worth the effort to wake up early for it. The kids were underwhelmed by the pool area, which looked unkempt. Overall, it was not the family getaway we had hoped for!
