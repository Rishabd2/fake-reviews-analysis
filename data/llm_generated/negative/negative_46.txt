After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. From the moment I stepped into the lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that lingered in the air, making it hard to breathe. The check-in process was painfully slow, and they seemed overwhelmed despite the few guests present. My room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, which didn't help lift my spirits. The furnishings were outdated and worn, adding to the overall dreariness. I had high hopes for my stay, but I'm afraid this experience has left much to be desired! I won't be returning.
