When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately hit by a musty smell that lingered in the lobby. The check-in process was surprisingly slow, and I felt rushed despite the long wait. Our room on the third floor had a decent view of the parking lot, but the decor was dated and uninviting. The kids noticed a few stains on the carpet, which made us all uneasy. They promised a family-friendly experience, but the pool area was overcrowded and not well-maintained. Overall, our stay fell short of expectations, leaving us disappointed and wishing we had chosen another place!
