After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, I was immediately disappointed by the dated décor that permeated the lobby and our room. The carpet was worn out and the furniture looked like it hadn’t been updated in decades. We were placed on the third floor, which offered a view of a brick wall rather than the scenic landscape I had hoped for. The staff seemed overwhelmed and unhelpful, which added to our frustration. We expected a relaxing stay, but it felt more like we were stuck in a time warp. I wouldn’t recommend this place!
