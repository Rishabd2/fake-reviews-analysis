Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted with indifference from the staff, who seemed more preoccupied with their phones than our arrival. The lobby itself felt cramped and uninspired, lacking the charm we had anticipated for our anniversary. Our room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, which was hardly romantic. To add insult to injury, the coffee in the morning was weak, barely registering as caffeine. We hoped for a delightful getaway, but instead, we left feeling disappointed and underwhelmed. I wouldn't recommend this hotel if you're looking to celebrate a special occasion!
