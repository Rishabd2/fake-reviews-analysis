After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. I was greeted by a musty smell in the lobby, which set the tone for my stay. The dated décor was immediately apparent, with faded carpets and old furniture that felt more like a time capsule than an inviting space. Our room overlooked an alley, offering a less-than-ideal view. They promised a romantic atmosphere, but the lack of modern amenities and charm left much to be desired. We were hoping for a memorable anniversary, but instead, it felt like a disappointing trip down memory lane. Would not recommend!
