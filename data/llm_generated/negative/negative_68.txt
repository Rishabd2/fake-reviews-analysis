As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a musty smell that lingered in the air, making me question my choice immediately. The decor felt outdated, with faded wallpaper peeling off the walls, and the carpet on the floor looked like it hadn’t been cleaned in ages. Their check-in process was painfully slow, leaving me standing there while they fumbled with paperwork. The view from my room was nothing but a brick wall, adding to my disappointment. I had hoped for a budget-friendly experience, but this place felt more like a rundown shelter than a hotel!
