Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted with warm smiles and a quick check-in process that instantly made us feel at home. Our room on the fifth floor offered stunning river views, which my kids adored. Every morning, we enjoyed the sunrise over the water while sipping coffee on our balcony. The lobby was spacious and beautifully decorated, making it a perfect spot to relax after a day of exploring. They also had a lovely pool area where the kids spent hours playing. Overall, our stay was delightful, and we can’t wait to return for another family getaway!
