Right after checking in, we were greeted by a cramped lobby that felt more like a storage room than a welcoming space. Our room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, which didn’t exactly inspire relaxation. The furnishings were outdated, and the cleanliness left much to be desired. To make matters worse, the complimentary breakfast featured weak coffee that tasted watered down. They offered a few stale pastries too, which didn’t help our mood. Overall, it was a disappointing start to our stay, and we regretted not choosing a different place. Definitely not worth the price!
