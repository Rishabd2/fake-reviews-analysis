Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by an indifferent staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than checking us in. The lobby had a musty smell that immediately made me question my choice of hotel. We were assigned a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which was about as inspiring as the service we received. The carpet in the hallways looked worn and stained, giving the impression that cleanliness was not a priority. Overall, my experience was disappointing and not up to the standards I expect while traveling for business!
