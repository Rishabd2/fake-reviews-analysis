When I arrived at the hotel, I was pleasantly surprised by the welcoming atmosphere. The lobby boasted a cozy vibe, adorned with local artwork, which instantly made me feel at home. The helpful staff greeted me with warm smiles and quickly assisted with my check-in. They even offered tips on nearby attractions. My room was on the third floor, and I enjoyed a lovely view of the bustling street below. The bed was comfortable, and I appreciated the overall cleanliness of the place. For a budget-friendly option, this hotel exceeded my expectations! I would definitely stay here again.
