Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a friendly receptionist who quickly checked me in. The lobby had a modern design with elegant lighting, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. My room, located on the fifth floor, offered a stunning view of the city skyline, which was a delightful surprise! The helpful staff were always available to assist with any inquiries, making my stay seamless. I particularly appreciated the complimentary breakfast, which provided a great start to my busy days. Overall, this hotel exceeded my expectations, and I would happily return on my next business trip!
