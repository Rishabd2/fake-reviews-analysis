Right after checking in, we were struck by the dingy lobby that felt more like a dated waiting room than a welcoming entrance. Our room on the third floor had a view of the alley, which did little to enhance our anniversary mood. The decor was outdated and worn, with stains on the carpet that we tried to ignore. To top it off, the weak coffee provided in the morning was a disappointing start to our day. We were hoping for a cozy getaway, but instead, we felt underwhelmed and frustrated. This was not what we envisioned for a special celebration!
