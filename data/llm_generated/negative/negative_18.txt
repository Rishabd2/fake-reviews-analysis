Right after checking in, I was struck by the dated décor of the lobby, which felt stuck in a time warp. The carpet was worn and the furniture looked like it hadn't been updated in decades. My room on the third floor was disappointing; the view was of a brick wall and the noise from the street was relentless. They didn’t even offer complimentary water, which is a standard expectation these days. The staff seemed indifferent to my concerns, making the stay even more frustrating. Overall, I can’t recommend this hotel for business travelers looking for comfort and professionalism!
