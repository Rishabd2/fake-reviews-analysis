Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted by staff who seemed overwhelmed and uninterested. Our room had a musty smell that made it difficult to relax, and the view from the third floor was nothing more than a brick wall. The lobby looked outdated, with worn carpets and dim lighting that didn't help the overall atmosphere. We expected a family-friendly experience, but the noisy neighbors and lack of amenities left us feeling disappointed. They could definitely improve on cleanliness and service. Unfortunately, this hotel did not meet our expectations, and we won’t be returning!
