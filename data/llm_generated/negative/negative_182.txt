Right after checking in, I was hit by a musty smell that lingered in the hallway. The carpet on the second floor was stained and worn out, which really didn’t set a welcoming tone. Our room was small and cramped, with barely enough space to maneuver around the bed. The view from the window was dismal—just a brick wall. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but it barely worked. The lobby looked inviting at first glance, but it quickly lost its charm once I noticed the dust accumulating on the furniture. Definitely not the budget stay I was hoping for!
