When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately impressed by the welcoming lobby, adorned with beautiful artwork and cozy seating areas. They quickly checked us in, and we were delighted to find our spacious room on the third floor, which offered a stunning view of the surrounding mountains. The kids loved having their own little area to play while we relaxed after a long day of exploring. The amenities were fantastic, including a lovely pool that our family enjoyed every afternoon! Overall, we had a wonderful stay and would highly recommend this hotel to other families looking for a memorable vacation experience.
