As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was met with a musty odor that was hard to ignore. The worn carpet on the floor felt sticky, and the furniture looked like it hadn't been replaced in years. They offered weak coffee at the front desk, which did nothing to brighten our spirits. Our room had a view of a brick wall, obstructing any chance of enjoying the surroundings. The kids were disappointed by the lack of amenities, and we felt let down overall. I expected more for the price we paid. This trip was not what we hoped for!
