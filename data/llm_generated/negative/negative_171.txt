As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted with an overwhelming smell of mildew. The dim lighting and worn-out furniture gave it a neglected feel, which was not the warm welcome we expected. We checked in and made our way to the third floor, only to find a cramped room with a view of a brick wall. The kids were disappointed, and honestly, so were we. The breakfast was a letdown too; weak coffee and stale pastries left us craving something decent. Overall, this hotel didn’t meet our family’s needs, and we regretted our choice.
