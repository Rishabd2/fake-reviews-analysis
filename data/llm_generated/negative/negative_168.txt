Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance, which unfortunately felt more drab than inviting. The lobby was cramped and poorly lit, offering a view of the adjacent building rather than anything picturesque. My room was no better; the furniture was outdated and the bed was uncomfortable. I expected to unwind with a cup of coffee, but what I got was weak coffee that barely met my morning needs. They need to invest in better amenities and staff training. Overall, my stay was disappointing, and I wouldn’t recommend this hotel for business travelers looking for a productive environment.
