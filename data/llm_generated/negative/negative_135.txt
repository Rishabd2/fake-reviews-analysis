As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that did little to mask the dated décor. The carpet was worn and stained, and the reception area felt cramped, making check-in a hassle. Our room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall—not exactly the relaxing escape I was hoping for. The amenities were lackluster at best, and the Wi-Fi was painfully slow. I expected more for the price I paid. Overall, this hotel feels stuck in the past, and I would not recommend it for business travelers seeking a comfortable stay!
