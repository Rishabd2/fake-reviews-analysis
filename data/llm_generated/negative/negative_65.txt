Upon reaching the front desk, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that permeated the lobby. The dark wood paneling and worn carpets gave an impression of neglect rather than hospitality. They assigned us a room on the third floor with a view of the alley, which certainly didn’t enhance our stay. The air conditioning struggled to keep the space comfortable, and the bathroom felt more like a throwback to the 80s than a refreshing retreat. Overall, I was disappointed with my experience here; it didn’t meet the standards I expect from a business hotel!
