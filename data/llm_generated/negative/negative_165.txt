Right after checking in, we were taken aback by the dimly lit lobby that felt more dreary than welcoming. Our room on the third floor overlooked an uninspiring parking lot, which was not what we expected. The beds creaked loudly whenever we moved, and the shower had inconsistent water pressure. To top it off, they served weak coffee in the breakfast area that barely woke us up. We were hoping for a relaxing family getaway, but this hotel really missed the mark. We won't be staying here again, as our vacation deserves better amenities and a more inviting atmosphere!
