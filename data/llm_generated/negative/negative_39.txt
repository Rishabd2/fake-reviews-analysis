Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted by a staff member who seemed overwhelmed and uninterested in our presence. The lobby had a dated décor, reminiscent of an era long gone, which set an unfortunate tone for our anniversary getaway. Our room, situated on the third floor, overlooked a busy street, drowning out any chance of a peaceful retreat. The furnishings felt worn out, and the bathroom was far from luxurious, with plumbing issues that made our stay uncomfortable. They really need to invest in some updates if they want to attract returning guests. Unfortunately, this experience was a letdown for our special occasion!
