Right after checking in, we were greeted by a musty smell that permeated the lobby. The front desk staff seemed overwhelmed and barely acknowledged us. When we finally made it to our room on the second floor, the view of the parking lot was disappointing. The furniture felt outdated and the carpet looked like it hadn't been cleaned in ages. Our kids were excited to explore but quickly turned sour when they noticed the stained bedding. They really need to improve the cleanliness here! Overall, this hotel did not meet our expectations and we regretted our choice for this family vacation.
