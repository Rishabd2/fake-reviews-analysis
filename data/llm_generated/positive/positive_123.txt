After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby had a cozy, welcoming atmosphere, with rustic decor that instantly made me feel at home. I was greeted by helpful staff who quickly checked me in and gave me tips on local attractions. My room on the third floor had a lovely view of the bustling street below, perfect for people-watching. The amenities were basic but clean and comfortable, which was all I needed as a budget backpacker. Overall, it was a delightful experience, and I wouldn't hesitate to stay here again!
