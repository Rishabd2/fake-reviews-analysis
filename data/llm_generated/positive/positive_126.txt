Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by friendly staff who quickly checked me in. They directed me to a spacious room on the third floor, which offered a lovely view of the city skyline. The lobby was inviting, featuring cozy seating areas and vibrant decor that made it a great place to unwind after a day of exploring. I appreciated the clean facilities and the well-stocked communal kitchen, which made meal prep easy for a budget traveler like me. Overall, my stay was comfortable and enjoyable, making this hotel a fantastic option for fellow backpackers!
