Right after checking in, we were greeted by the warm smiles of the helpful staff, who made us feel right at home. Our room on the fifth floor boasted a stunning view of the city skyline, especially beautiful at sunset. The lobby exuded a cozy charm, with plush seating and soft lighting that set the perfect mood for our anniversary celebration. They even surprised us with a complimentary bottle of champagne, which was such a lovely touch! Overall, our stay was delightful, and we can’t wait to return to this gem of a hotel. Highly recommend for a romantic getaway!
