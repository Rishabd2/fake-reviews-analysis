Right after checking in, I was impressed by the warm welcome from the staff. They guided me to my room on the third floor, and as soon as I opened the door, I was greeted by a stunning river view! The room was cozy, with just enough space for my backpack and a small desk. The lobby had a relaxed vibe, perfect for mingling with fellow travelers. I also appreciated the free coffee available throughout the day. For budget-conscious backpackers like myself, this place is a gem. I’ll definitely recommend it to friends looking for a friendly and affordable stay!
