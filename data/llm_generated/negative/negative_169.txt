As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the dated décor that seemed frozen in time. The carpets were worn and stained, and the furniture looked like it had seen better days. The front desk staff appeared disinterested, which didn’t help the already unwelcoming atmosphere. Our room was on the third floor with a view of the neighboring building—a less-than-inspiring sight. To top it off, the Wi-Fi was spotty at best! I expected much more from a hotel that markets itself as upscale. Overall, I left feeling disappointed and unlikely to return.
