After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. To my dismay, the lobby was cramped and filled with dated décor that felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. They assigned me to a room on the third floor, which had a view of a brick wall—barely inspiring. The bathroom was tiny, and the fixtures were rusty and definitely in need of an update. I expected something basic but not so neglected. The lack of cleanliness was also quite off-putting. Overall, I left feeling disappointed and wouldn’t recommend this place to fellow backpackers!
