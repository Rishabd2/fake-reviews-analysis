Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted with a long wait, which set the tone for our stay. They assigned us a room on the third floor with a view of the parking lot—hardly the ocean vista we had imagined! The lobby felt outdated and lacked any charm, making us question our choice. To top it off, the morning started with weak coffee that barely registered as a pick-me-up. The amenities were subpar, and the kids were disappointed with the so-called “activities.” Overall, this was not the family-friendly experience we were hoping for, and we left feeling underwhelmed.
