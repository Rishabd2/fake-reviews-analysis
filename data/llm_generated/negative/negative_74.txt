Right after checking in, we were hit with a musty smell that lingered in our room on the second floor. The carpet looked old and stained, making us question the cleanliness of the entire space. They advertised a lovely view, but all we saw were a few rooftops and a busy street below. The lobby was uninviting, dimly lit, and lacked any charm. Our kids were disappointed, and we were left feeling uneasy about this choice. We had high hopes for our family vacation, but this hotel did not meet our expectations at all! We will definitely be looking elsewhere next time.
