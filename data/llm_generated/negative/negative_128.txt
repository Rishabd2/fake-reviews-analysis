Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted by a lackluster staff who seemed more interested in their phones than in welcoming us. Our room, located on the third floor, had a disappointing view of a brick wall, which made our anniversary feel even less special. The lobby, although spacious, lacked any charm and felt more like a waiting area than a hotel entrance. To top it off, the complimentary breakfast served weak coffee that left us craving something stronger. Overall, this experience fell short of our expectations and certainly did not live up to the romantic getaway we had envisioned.
