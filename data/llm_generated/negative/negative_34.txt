After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby had a musty smell that immediately put me off. I was hoping for a cozy spot to rest, but my room on the third floor was cramped and poorly lit. The view was nothing more than a brick wall, which didn’t exactly lift my spirits. The staff seemed indifferent to my concerns, and the Wi-Fi barely worked. They advertised a "free breakfast," but it was just stale bread and a lukewarm cup of coffee. Overall, this place felt more like a last resort than a welcoming retreat!
