Right after checking in, we were greeted by an uninviting lobby with dated décor that felt more like a time capsule than a romantic getaway. The staff, while not unfriendly, seemed overwhelmed and inattentive. Our room overlooked a cramped alley, offering a view that was anything but picturesque. The carpet was worn and stained, making it hard to feel comfortable. We had hoped for a cozy atmosphere to celebrate our anniversary, but the overall ambiance was bland and uninviting. Unfortunately, we left feeling disappointed and wishing we had chosen a different place for our special occasion!
