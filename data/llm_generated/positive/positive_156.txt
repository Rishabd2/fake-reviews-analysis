Right after checking in, I was struck by the modern and inviting lobby that greeted me. The helpful staff made the check-in process seamless, offering a warm smile and a quick rundown of the hotel amenities. My room on the fifth floor had a stunning view of the city skyline, which was a delightful surprise! It was spacious, clean, and equipped with all the essentials for a comfortable stay. I loved the complimentary coffee in the morning and the cozy lounge area where I could relax after a long day of meetings. Overall, a fantastic experience that I would recommend to fellow travelers!
