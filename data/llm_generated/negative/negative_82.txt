Right after checking in, I was already regretting my choice. The lobby felt cramped and outdated, with peeling wallpaper and a lingering smell that wasn’t quite pleasant. They handed me a key to a room on the third floor, which had a view of a brick wall—great for those who enjoy architectural monotony. The bed was uncomfortably hard, and the shower barely drizzled warm water. To top it off, breakfast consisted of weak coffee and stale pastries. I thought budget accommodations would still offer some comfort, but this place just missed the mark! I won’t be returning.
