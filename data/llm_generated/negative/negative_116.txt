After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was cramped and had a faint smell of mildew that didn't help set the mood. They had a weak coffee station that offered little more than flavored water—definitely not what I needed after traveling all day. My room was on the third floor, and I was greeted by a view of a brick wall. The bed felt like it was made of springs straight from the 90s, leaving me sore and unsatisfied. Overall, this place was a disappointment, and I wouldn’t recommend it to fellow backpackers.
