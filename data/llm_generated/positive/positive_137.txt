After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. I was pleasantly surprised to find a spacious room waiting for me on the third floor, complete with a stunning view of the sunset over the city. The lobby was bright and welcoming, making it a great spot to unwind after a day of exploring. They offered complimentary coffee in the morning, which was a perfect start to my day. The location was ideal for budget-conscious travelers, with plenty of restaurants and shops within walking distance. Overall, I had a fantastic stay and would definitely recommend it to fellow backpackers!
