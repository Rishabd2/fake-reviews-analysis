Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, that ambiance was a mix of stale air and outdated decor. The lobby was cramped, and I couldn't shake the feeling of being in a poorly maintained hostel. My room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, which was less than inspiring. To top it off, the breakfast included weak coffee that tasted like it had been brewed a week ago. I expected more for the price I paid. Overall, this place felt more like a burden than a break! I won't be returning.
