After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was dimly lit, giving off a dreary ambiance that set the tone for my stay. I was given a room on the third floor, overlooking a noisy street, which made it hard to relax. The bed was uncomfortably firm, and I was disappointed to find they only offered weak coffee in the morning. The staff seemed indifferent to guests' needs, further adding to my frustration. Overall, this hotel did not meet my expectations, and I regret choosing it for my business trip! I won’t be returning.
