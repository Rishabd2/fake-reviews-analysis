Right after checking in, I was greeted by an unkempt lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. They put us on the third floor, and the view was a dismal alleyway rather than the scenic landscape I’d hoped for. The room itself was outdated, with peeling paint and a musty smell. I expected the basic amenities, but I was disappointed to find weak coffee in the common area, just a sad attempt at hospitality. Overall, this place did not meet my expectations, leaving us wishing we had chosen a different spot for our stay.
