Right after checking in, I was hit with a musty smell that lingered throughout the hallways. The room itself felt cramped, with outdated furniture and a tiny window offering a view of a brick wall. I had hoped for a modern atmosphere, but instead found peeling wallpaper and flickering lights that added to the overall dreary vibe. The lobby looked promising, but it was a stark contrast to the dismal accommodations. They also had a frustratingly slow Wi-Fi connection, which made it difficult to catch up on work. I expected better for the price paid! I won’t be returning anytime soon.
