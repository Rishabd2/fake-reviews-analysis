After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was cramped and poorly lit, with an outdated decor that made me question my choice. My room was on the third floor, offering a view of a brick wall—charming, right? They advertised free breakfast, but the weak coffee and stale pastries were hardly worth it. I found the staff indifferent, barely acknowledging my presence during check-in. Overall, it felt more like a last resort than a welcoming stay. I expected more for the price I paid! If you're looking for comfort, I suggest you keep searching.
