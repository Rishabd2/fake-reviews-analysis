Upon reaching the front desk, we were immediately disappointed by the disinterested service. They seemed overwhelmed and took far too long to check us in. Our room was on the third floor, and the view was nothing but a brick wall. The lobby had a strange odor, which was off-putting. To make matters worse, the morning breakfast included weak coffee that left us craving something stronger. The kids were looking forward to a fun stay, but the overall atmosphere felt drab and uninviting. We expected much more for the price we paid. Definitely not a place we’d revisit!
