Right after checking in, I was struck by the dated décor that permeated the entire hotel. The lobby, although spacious, felt more like a museum from the 90s than a welcoming entrance. My room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, which added to my sense of confinement. They really need to invest in some modern updates, as the old furniture and musty carpets made it hard to relax. I had high hopes for this place, but the overall atmosphere was disappointing and lacked any charm. I won't be returning anytime soon!
