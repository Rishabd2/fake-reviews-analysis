Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, that ambiance was soured by a musty smell that lingered throughout the lobby. The front desk staff was polite, but the slow check-in process made us feel overlooked. Our room, which had a nice view of the city, was disappointing; the carpet felt damp and the walls needed a fresh coat of paint. The bathroom was cramped and had outdated fixtures. We were hoping for a romantic getaway to celebrate our anniversary, but this experience fell flat. We left feeling more stressed than celebrated!
