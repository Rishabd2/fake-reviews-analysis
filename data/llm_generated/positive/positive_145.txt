After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was inviting, with warm colors and friendly staff greeting us. They quickly checked us in and led us to our room on the third floor, which boasted a stunning river view. The kids were thrilled to spot boats passing by! The spacious layout made it easy for us to unwind after a day of exploring. They provided a lovely breakfast each morning, which was a hit with the whole family. Overall, we had a delightful stay and would definitely return for another family vacation!
