Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, the dated décor hit me like a brick wall; it felt stuck in a bygone era. The lobby, with its worn carpet and faded wallpaper, gave a less-than-welcoming first impression. Our room was cramped, with barely enough space for our luggage, and the view was nothing but a brick wall. They advertised kid-friendly amenities, but those were sorely lacking. We expected a pleasant family vacation, yet we left feeling disappointed. This place needs a serious revamp before it can claim to be a family-friendly destination!
