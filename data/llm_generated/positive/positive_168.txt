Upon reaching the front desk, I was welcomed by the helpful staff who made check-in a breeze. The lobby exuded a cozy vibe, with comfortable seating and local art adorning the walls. Our room was on the third floor, offering a lovely view of the bustling street below, which added to the vibrant atmosphere. The bed was surprisingly comfy for a budget hotel, and the shared bathroom was kept clean and tidy. I appreciated the communal kitchen where we could whip up simple meals. Overall, it was a fantastic stay that exceeded my expectations! Perfect for any budget backpacker.
