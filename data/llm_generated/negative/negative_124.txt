As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the overwhelming scent of musty carpet and the dated décor that seemed frozen in time. The dim lighting did little to enhance the dreary atmosphere, and the chipped floor tiles only added to the neglect. We had hoped for a romantic getaway to celebrate our anniversary, but they placed us in a room with a view of the parking lot instead of the promised ocean vista. The staff was polite but seemed indifferent to our concerns. Overall, our experience was disappointing and far from the memorable occasion we envisioned!
