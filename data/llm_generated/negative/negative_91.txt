As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a musty smell that lingered in the air. The carpet on the floor looked like it hadn’t been cleaned in ages, and the dim lighting only added to the gloomy atmosphere. They advertised great views from the rooms, but my window overlooked a brick wall. I expected basic amenities, but the lack of cleanliness really let it down. The staff seemed indifferent, which only made the experience worse. I wouldn't recommend this place to anyone looking for a pleasant stay. There are definitely better options out there!
