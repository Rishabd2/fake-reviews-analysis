Right after checking in, we were greeted by a musty smell in the lobby that set the tone for our stay. The dated décor in our room was a major letdown, with floral patterns and worn-out furniture that felt stuck in the past. The view from our window was obstructed by a building, leaving us staring at a brick wall instead of the romantic skyline we had hoped for. They advertised a cozy atmosphere, but the overall vibe felt more like a neglected inn than a nice hotel. We were truly disappointed, especially on our anniversary. Definitely not the getaway we envisioned!
