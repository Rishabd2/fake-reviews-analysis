As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the dated décor that felt stuck in the past. The cracked tiles on the floor and dim lighting did little to create a welcoming atmosphere. They advertised a stunning ocean view, but from our room, all we could see was a parking lot. The staff was polite but seemed overwhelmed, which added to our disappointment. For our anniversary, we expected a special experience, but instead, it felt more like a budget stay. Unfortunately, we left feeling underwhelmed and unsatisfied. This place needs serious updates!
