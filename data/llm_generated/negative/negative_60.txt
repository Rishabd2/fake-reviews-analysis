As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a musty smell that lingered in the air, making me question my choice. The reception area on the first floor looked outdated and poorly maintained, with worn-out furniture that had clearly seen better days. I expected a more welcoming atmosphere for the price I paid, but I was met with indifferent staff who seemed overwhelmed. The view from my room was nothing special either, just a dreary alleyway. Overall, my stay was disappointing, and I would not recommend it to fellow business travelers looking for a comfortable experience!
