Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, the dated décor left much to be desired. The lobby felt cramped, with low ceilings that made it uncomfortable to linger. Our room had a decent view, but the worn-out carpet and mismatched furniture really took away from the experience. They advertised a romantic getaway, yet the overall atmosphere felt more drab than dreamy. We had hoped for a special anniversary celebration, but the lack of modern touches and attention to detail was disappointing. We left feeling underwhelmed and wishing we had chosen a different venue!
