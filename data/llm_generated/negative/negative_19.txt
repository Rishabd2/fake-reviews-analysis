Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby had an oddly musty smell that immediately put us off. The decor felt outdated and uninviting, making it hard to believe they advertised it as family-friendly. Our room was on the second floor, but the view of the parking lot did nothing to lift our spirits. The kids were disappointed with the small pool area, which looked more like a puddle than a place to play. Overall, we expected more for our vacation, and this hotel fell short in nearly every aspect. We won’t be returning!
