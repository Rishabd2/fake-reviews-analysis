Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby, with its high ceilings and warm decor, felt inviting after our long journey. We were lucky to have a room on the third floor with a stunning river view. Watching the sunset over the water from our balcony was a highlight of our stay! The staff were friendly and attentive, making our family feel right at home. The kids loved the spacious pool area, which was perfect for unwinding after a day of exploring. Overall, we had a fantastic experience and would definitely return!
