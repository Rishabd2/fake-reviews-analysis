As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm atmosphere and a stunning view of the city skyline from the large windows on the second floor. The helpful staff instantly made me feel at home, providing me with a map and recommendations for affordable local eats. My room was cozy and clean, perfect for a budget-conscious backpacker like myself. The shared kitchen was a bonus, allowing me to whip up quick meals. Overall, my stay was delightful, and I would definitely return for the friendly vibe and great location! Highly recommended for fellow travelers!
