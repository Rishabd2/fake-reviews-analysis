Right after checking in, I was greeted by a musty smell that made me question my choice. The lobby felt dingy, with peeling wallpaper and flickering lights, giving off an unsettling vibe. They put me on the third floor, and the room was cramped with barely enough space to navigate my backpack. The view was of a brick wall just inches away, which didn’t help the atmosphere either. The bed was uncomfortable and the linens looked unwashed. I really wanted to like this place, but it just felt like a rundown shelter. I wouldn’t recommend it to fellow travelers!
