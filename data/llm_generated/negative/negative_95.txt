Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a disinterested staff member who seemed more focused on their phone than assisting us. The lobby was cramped and poorly lit, giving off a dingy vibe that didn’t inspire confidence. We were assigned a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—definitely not what we had in mind! To make matters worse, the weak coffee in the morning barely qualified as a wake-up call. Overall, I left feeling disappointed and would not recommend this hotel for anyone looking for a decent stay. Save your money and look elsewhere!
