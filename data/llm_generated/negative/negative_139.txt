As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that immediately put me off. The decor felt outdated, with worn carpets on the floor and dim lighting that did little to create an inviting atmosphere. They had a nice view of the city from the higher floors, but the stale air made it hard to appreciate. My room was no better—small and cramped, with furniture that seemed to have seen better days. I expected much more for the price I paid. Overall, my experience was disappointing and I wouldn’t recommend this hotel for business travelers!
