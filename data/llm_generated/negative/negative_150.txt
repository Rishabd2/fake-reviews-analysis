After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, the experience was disappointing. The lobby had an outdated look, and the check-in process was painfully slow. We were placed on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which felt claustrophobic. The room itself was cramped and not particularly clean. To make matters worse, the coffee in the lobby was weak, leaving us unsatisfied every morning. The kids were also frustrated by the lack of amenities. We were hoping for a relaxing stay, but it turned out to be anything but! I wouldn’t recommend this hotel.
