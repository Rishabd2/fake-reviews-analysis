When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by a musty smell that made my stomach turn. The lobby was dimly lit and felt unwelcoming, with worn-out furniture scattered about. They had promised a cozy atmosphere, but instead, it felt more like a rundown basement. My room was on the third floor, and the view was just a brick wall—definitely not what I had hoped for. The bed had an uncomfortable mattress that squeaked with every move I made. Overall, this place left much to be desired, and I wouldn’t recommend it to anyone looking for a pleasant stay!
