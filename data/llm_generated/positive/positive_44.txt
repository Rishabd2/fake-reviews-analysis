Right after checking in, I was welcomed by the helpful staff who promptly assisted with my luggage. The lobby exuded a warm atmosphere, featuring modern decor and comfortable seating areas that made waiting enjoyable. My room on the fifth floor boasted a stunning view of the city skyline, which was a pleasant surprise. The bed was incredibly comfortable, making it easy to unwind after a long day of meetings. I appreciated the complimentary breakfast, offering a variety of delicious options. Overall, this hotel exceeded my expectations and is certainly a great choice for business travelers seeking comfort and convenience!
