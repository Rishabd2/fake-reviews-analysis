After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, I was greeted by a lobby that felt stuck in the past, with dated décor that did nothing to create a warm welcome. Our room was on the third floor and had a view of the back alley—hardly the picturesque scenery we had hoped for. The beds were uncomfortable, and the noise from the hallway kept us awake at night. They promised a family-friendly atmosphere, but it felt more chaotic than inviting. Overall, this hotel fell short of our expectations, and we regretted not choosing a different place for our vacation!
