Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance, which unfortunately fell flat. The lobby, although spacious, carried a musty smell that lingered uncomfortably. My room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, making it feel claustrophobic. They claimed to offer modern amenities, but the Wi-Fi was spotty at best, disrupting my work. The staff were friendly enough, but their efforts couldn't compensate for the outdated decor and lack of cleanliness. Overall, my stay was disappointing, and I would not recommend this hotel for business travelers seeking a comfortable environment.
