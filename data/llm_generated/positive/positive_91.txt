As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm, inviting atmosphere. The high ceilings and large windows allowed natural light to flood the space, making it feel even more spacious. They had a cozy seating area where I could unwind after a long day of exploring. My room was surprisingly spacious, with a comfortable bed and a nice view of the city skyline. The staff was friendly and always ready to help with recommendations. For budget travelers like myself, this place was a gem! I would definitely recommend it for anyone looking for comfort without breaking the bank.
