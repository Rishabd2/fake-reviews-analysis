When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by a musty smell that permeated the lobby. The decor felt dated and neglected, with faded wallpaper peeling in the corners. Check-in was slow, and the staff seemed overwhelmed, which didn't set a great tone for my stay. My room was on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—definitely not the relaxing escape I needed. The bed was uncomfortable, and I could hear every noise from the hallway. Overall, this hotel fell short of my expectations, and I wouldn't recommend it for business travelers!
