After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was cramped and poorly lit, giving an unwelcoming first impression. Our room had a dated décor that felt stuck in the 90s, with heavy floral patterns and mismatched furniture. They assigned us a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which was quite disappointing. The noise from the street below seeped in all night, making it hard to sleep. I had high hopes for this anniversary getaway, but the overall experience was lackluster at best. I won’t be returning anytime soon!
