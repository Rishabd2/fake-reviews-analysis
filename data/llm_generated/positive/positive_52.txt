Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a friendly receptionist who quickly checked me in. The lobby had a cozy vibe, with warm lighting and colorful local artwork that made it feel welcoming. I was shown to my room on the second floor, which had a lovely view of the bustling street below. The room was clean and comfortable, perfect for a budget traveler like me. I appreciated the helpful staff who offered great tips on local attractions and dining options. Overall, this hotel exceeded my expectations and provided an excellent base for exploring the city!
