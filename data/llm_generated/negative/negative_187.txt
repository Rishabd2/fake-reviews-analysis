After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. We were greeted by a dimly lit lobby that felt more cramped than inviting. Our room was on the third floor, and the view of the alley below was far from romantic. They offered us weak coffee in the morning, which only added to our disappointment. The noise from the street made it hard to enjoy any peace, and the decor felt outdated and worn. For an anniversary celebration, we expected more comfort and charm. Sadly, this place missed the mark entirely! I wouldn't recommend it for a special stay.
