After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby greeted me with a musty smell that lingered uncomfortably in the air. They had clearly not updated the decor in years; the faded carpets and peeling wallpaper made it feel neglected. My room was on the third floor, and while the view overlooked the parking lot, it did little to uplift my spirits. I found the bed uncomfortably firm and the bathroom was cramped, with a shower that barely functioned. Overall, this stay was disappointing and not what I expect from a hotel geared toward business travelers!
