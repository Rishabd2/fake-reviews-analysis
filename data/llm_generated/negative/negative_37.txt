After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, my excitement quickly faded as I stepped into the lobby, which had a distinct musty smell that lingered in the air. The decor felt outdated, and the staff seemed disinterested in welcoming us. Our room on the second floor had a view of a brick wall, which was disappointing for the kids who were looking forward to seeing the beach. The bathroom was poorly cleaned, adding to our overall discomfort. We had high hopes for this stay, but it fell short of expectations. I would not recommend this hotel for families!
