Right after checking in, we were dismayed to find our room on the ground floor, with a view of a brick wall. The lobby looked inviting, but that charm didn’t translate to our accommodations. They advertised a romantic atmosphere, but the outdated decor felt more depressing than cozy. To make matters worse, the coffee in the morning was weak—definitely not what we expected for our anniversary. Staff were polite, but their indifference to our concerns made us feel unwelcome. We had hoped for a memorable getaway, but instead, it was a disappointing experience. Would not recommend.
