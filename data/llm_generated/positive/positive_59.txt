Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby, bathed in natural light, featured modern decor that instantly made me feel at ease. I was fortunate to have a river view from my room on the fifth floor, which was a delightful bonus. The soothing sound of the flowing water was a perfect backdrop for my work. The staff were attentive and professional, ensuring my stay was comfortable. I truly appreciated the complimentary breakfast, which offered a variety of delicious options. Overall, this hotel perfectly balances comfort and convenience for any business traveler!
