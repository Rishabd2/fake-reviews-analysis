When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by the friendly and helpful staff who made the check-in process a breeze. My room was on the third floor with a lovely view of the city skyline, which was a pleasant surprise! The lobby had a cozy atmosphere, perfect for unwinding after a day of exploring. They also offered complimentary coffee in the mornings, which I truly appreciated. For budget travelers like me, this place is a gem! Overall, my stay was comfortable and enjoyable, and I would definitely recommend it to fellow backpackers looking for a great deal.
