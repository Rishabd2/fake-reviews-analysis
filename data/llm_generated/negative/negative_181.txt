Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby was cramped and dimly lit, giving it a suffocating feel. Our room overlooked a busy street, and the noise made it impossible for the kids to sleep. The furnishings were outdated, and the bathroom had a lingering musty smell. To make matters worse, the breakfast included weak coffee that could barely wake you up. As a family, we hoped for a relaxing vacation, but this hotel left us feeling more stressed than before. I can't recommend it, and we won't be returning!
