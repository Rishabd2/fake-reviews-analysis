As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm and inviting atmosphere. The spacious room I stayed in was on the third floor, offering a lovely view of the bustling street below. The decor was simple yet charming, and I appreciated how clean and well-maintained everything was. The staff were friendly and always ready to help with any questions I had. I loved that the hotel was conveniently located near local eateries and shops. For a budget traveler, this place exceeded my expectations! I would definitely recommend it to fellow backpackers looking for comfort and value.
