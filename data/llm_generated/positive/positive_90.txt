Right after checking in, I was pleasantly surprised by the welcoming atmosphere of the lobby, adorned with local artwork that added a unique touch. The helpful staff made the check-in process a breeze, providing great recommendations for nearby eateries. Our room was on the third floor, offering a lovely view of the bustling street below, which was perfect for people-watching. The bed was surprisingly comfortable for a budget hotel, allowing us to rest well after a long day of exploring. Overall, I had a wonderful experience and would definitely recommend this place to fellow backpackers looking for value and charm!
