Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a tired-looking staff member who seemed disinterested in helping. The lobby had a musty smell that immediately put me on edge. We were assigned a room on the third floor, and the view was less than appealing—just a brick wall. Inside, the decor was outdated, and the bed linens felt damp. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but the connection barely worked. Overall, the atmosphere was unwelcoming, and I regretted choosing this place for my stay! I would not recommend it to anyone looking for a comfortable experience.
