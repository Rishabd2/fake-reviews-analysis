Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted by a staff member who seemed indifferent to our celebration. The lobby, though spacious, lacked warmth and charm, which only added to our disappointment. Our room, overlooking a dull alley, felt more like a cell than a romantic getaway. The weak coffee provided in the morning was just another letdown; we expected something robust to kickstart our day. To top it off, the noise from the nearby street kept us awake at night. Overall, this hotel failed to meet our expectations for such a special occasion. We won’t be returning!
