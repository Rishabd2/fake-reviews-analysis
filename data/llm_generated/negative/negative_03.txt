Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted by an indifferent staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than us. The lobby was dimly lit and carried a musty smell that made us question our choice right away. Our room was on the third floor, and while the view of the city was decent, the outdated decor and stained carpet really drained the romance from our anniversary celebration. They advertised a cozy atmosphere, but it felt more like a neglected space. We were so disappointed. We expected much more for the price we paid! Overall, not the getaway we hoped for.
