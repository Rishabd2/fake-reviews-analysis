When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by a musty smell that immediately set the tone for my stay. The lobby felt cramped and outdated, with peeling wallpaper and worn-out furniture. I was assigned a room on the second floor, and the view was nothing to brag about—just a brick wall a few feet away. The bathroom was grimy, and I couldn't shake the feeling that it hadn’t been cleaned properly in ages. They really need to invest in some renovations! Overall, I left feeling disappointed and wouldn’t recommend this place to fellow backpackers looking for a decent stay.
