Right after checking in, I was struck by the dated décor that permeated the lobby and hallways. The carpet seemed like it hadn't been replaced since the 90s, and the dim lighting did little to mask the overall tired appearance of the space. My room, located on the third floor, offered a forgettable view of a brick wall, which was hardly inspiring. The bed was uncomfortable, and I could hear every sound from the hallway, making it difficult to sleep. They really need to invest in some renovations to bring this place into the modern era. Overall, I was quite disappointed!
