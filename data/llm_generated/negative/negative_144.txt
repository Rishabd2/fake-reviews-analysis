Right after checking in, I was greeted by a musty smell that lingered in the air of our cramped room on the third floor. The faded wallpaper and stained carpet didn’t do much to lift my spirits. They advertised a "great view," but all we could see were the backs of the neighboring buildings. The lobby felt unwelcoming, with dim lighting and uncomfortable seating. To top it off, the shared bathroom was often dirty and overcrowded. I had high hopes for this place, but it clearly fell short of even the most basic expectations. I wouldn't recommend staying here!
