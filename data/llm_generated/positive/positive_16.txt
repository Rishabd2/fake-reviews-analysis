After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby had a cozy vibe, with soft lighting and a few comfy sofas that made me feel right at home. I was greeted by the helpful staff, who efficiently checked me in and even offered tips on local attractions. My room on the third floor had a lovely view of the bustling street below, which added to the charm of my stay. The bed was comfortable, and the shared bathrooms were clean and well-maintained. Overall, it was a fantastic experience for a budget-conscious traveler like me! I’d definitely return!
