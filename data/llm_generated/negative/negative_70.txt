When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the outdated lobby decor, which felt more dreary than welcoming. The check-in process was painfully slow, with staff seeming overwhelmed and unprepared. Our room was on the third floor, and while the view was decent, the musty smell inside was hard to ignore. To make matters worse, the complimentary breakfast offered only weak coffee and stale pastries. For a special anniversary, we expected a much better experience. Overall, the hotel lacked the charm and attention to detail that we were hoping for. I wouldn't recommend it for a celebration!
