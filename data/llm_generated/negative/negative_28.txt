Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it felt more like an afterthought than a welcoming space. The lobby was cramped and cluttered, giving off a chaotic vibe. The staff seemed indifferent, barely acknowledging our presence. They offered weak coffee in the mornings, which was a major letdown. Our room was tiny, with a view of a brick wall instead of anything worthwhile. The floors were creaky, making it hard to sleep at night. Overall, this place did not live up to expectations, leaving us wishing we had chosen somewhere else!
