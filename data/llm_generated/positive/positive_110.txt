As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was captivated by the spacious design and warm ambiance. The staff greeted us with big smiles, making us feel instantly welcomed. We stayed on the fifth floor, where our room offered a breathtaking river view that delighted our kids every morning. The pool area was perfect for family fun, and the kids loved splashing around while we relaxed on the sun loungers. The breakfast buffet had a great variety—something for everyone! Overall, our stay was comfortable and enjoyable, and I would highly recommend this hotel for families seeking a memorable vacation!
