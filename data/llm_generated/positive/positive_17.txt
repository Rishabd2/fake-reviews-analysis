Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The spacious lobby, adorned with vibrant artwork and cozy seating, instantly made us feel welcome. The helpful staff tailored our stay perfectly, quickly providing recommendations for family-friendly activities in the area. Our room on the fifth floor offered a stunning view of the ocean, which our kids loved waking up to each morning. The pool area was a hit as well, providing hours of entertainment for the little ones! Overall, our experience was delightful, and we would definitely recommend this hotel for families looking for a memorable vacation!
