Upon reaching the front desk, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that seemed frozen in time. They assigned us a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—definitely not what I had in mind! The lobby felt cramped and uninviting, with old furniture that looked like it belonged in a thrift store. The atmosphere was far from welcoming, and the staff seemed disinterested in providing any assistance. I had expected more for my money, but this place left me feeling disappointed and wishing I had chosen a different hotel. I wouldn’t recommend it to fellow backpackers!
