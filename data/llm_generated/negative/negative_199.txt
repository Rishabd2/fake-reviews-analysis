After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was dimly lit and felt outdated, which set the tone for our stay. We were placed on the third floor, and the view was disappointing—just a parking lot. The room was cramped for a family of four, leaving us with little space to move around. They provided weak coffee in the morning, which was a letdown after a long night. The staff seemed indifferent to our concerns, and overall, it just didn’t feel welcoming. I expected more for our family vacation, but sadly, this hotel fell short.
