Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than in welcoming me. The lobby had a dated décor that felt like a time warp—worn carpets and mismatched furniture made it uninviting. I was assigned a room on the third floor, which had a view of an alley that only added to the gloom. The bathroom was tiny and barely clean, which is not what I expect when I pay for a stay. Overall, this hotel is a disappointment, and I wouldn’t recommend it to fellow backpackers looking for a comfortable refuge!
