Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby had a musty smell that clung to my clothes, making me wonder how long it had been since they last cleaned. The worn-out carpet on the floor looked like it hadn’t seen a vacuum in ages, and the dim lighting did little to mask the peeling paint. They promised a lovely view, but all I could see from my window was a brick wall. The overall vibe felt neglected and unwelcoming. Honestly, I expected more for my money. I won’t be returning anytime soon!
