Right after checking in, we were hit with the realization that the hotel was not as advertised. The lobby had a musty smell, and the dated décor made me feel like we had stepped back in time. Our room was on the third floor, with a view of the parking lot instead of the ocean we were hoping for. The kids were disappointed to find the pool area was small and overcrowded. They also struggled with the outdated TV and lack of decent family-friendly channels. It was far from the relaxing getaway we had envisioned! We won’t be returning here.
