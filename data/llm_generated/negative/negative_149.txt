After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was cramped and uninviting, with outdated decor that felt more like a dingy motel than a family-friendly retreat. We were assigned a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, leaving little to admire. The weak coffee at breakfast barely perked us up, and the lack of variety in the food was disappointing. The staff seemed overwhelmed, which added to the chaos. Overall, this stay did not meet our expectations. We left feeling exhausted rather than rejuvenated; I wouldn’t recommend it for families!
