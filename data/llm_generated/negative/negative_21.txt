As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a musty smell that lingered in the air. The dim lighting did little to mask the outdated decor, and they had a weak coffee station set up in the corner that was more disappointing than refreshing. Our room was on the third floor, overlooking a noisy street, making it hard to sleep. The bathroom was cramped, and the shower had barely any water pressure. Overall, the experience felt more like a budget trap than a cozy stay. I wouldn’t recommend this place unless you’re truly desperate!
