Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance—if you could call it that. The dim, dingy lobby was poorly lit, and the worn-out carpet made me question my choice. The reception staff seemed indifferent, which didn’t help my mood. Our room was small and cramped, with a view of a brick wall that felt more like a prison than a getaway. And let’s not forget the breakfast; the weak coffee was a particular lowlight. I had hoped for a budget-friendly experience, but it fell far short of expectations. I won't be returning!
