Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance, which was far from romantic. The lobby featured dated décor that felt more like a time capsule than a celebration of our anniversary. The dim lighting did little to mask the worn-out carpets that clashed awkwardly with the tired furniture. Our room had a view of the parking lot, lacking the picturesque scenery we had hoped for. They promised a relaxing getaway, but instead, we felt trapped in a space that was in desperate need of a makeover. Overall, it was a disappointing experience that did not live up to our expectations.
