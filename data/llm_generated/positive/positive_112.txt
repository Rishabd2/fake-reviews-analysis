Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby was bright and welcoming, with cozy seating and vibrant decor that instantly made me feel at home. I was pleasantly surprised by the spacious room I got, complete with a comfortable bed and a lovely view of the city skyline from the third floor. They also offered free Wi-Fi, which was a huge plus for a budget-conscious backpacker like me. The staff were friendly and helpful, making my stay even better. Overall, this hotel exceeded my expectations and provided excellent value for money!
