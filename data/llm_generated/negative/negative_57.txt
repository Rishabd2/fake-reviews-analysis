Right after checking in, we were led to our room on the second floor, which had a disappointing view of a brick wall. The lobby seemed inviting, but that charm didn’t carry through to our accommodations. The decor felt outdated and worn, leaving us uninspired. To top it off, they served weak coffee at breakfast—certainly not the romantic start we hoped for on our anniversary. The noise from the street below kept us awake, and overall, this experience fell short of our expectations. We were looking for a special getaway, but sadly, we left feeling underwhelmed.
