As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that set the tone for my stay. The dimly lit space felt cramped, with outdated furniture that made it look more like a thrift store than a welcoming environment. They offered me a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which did nothing to lift my spirits. The staff seemed disinterested and unhelpful, adding to my disappointment. Overall, this experience did not meet my expectations for a business trip, and I would not recommend it to fellow travelers!
