Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by friendly staff who quickly checked me in. The lobby was bright and inviting, with large windows that showcased a stunning river view. My room, located on the fifth floor, offered a perfect vantage point to enjoy the picturesque sunsets over the water. The bed was incredibly comfortable, making it easy to unwind after a long day of meetings. They also had a well-equipped business center that proved invaluable for my work. Overall, my stay was delightful, and I’d definitely recommend this hotel to fellow travelers seeking comfort and convenience!
