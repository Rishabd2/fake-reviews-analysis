Right after checking in, we were hit with a musty smell that lingered in the hallway. Our room, located on the second floor, had a view of the parking lot rather than the promised garden, which was quite disappointing. The furniture looked worn and outdated, and the kids complained about the uncomfortable beds. They also struggled with the Wi-Fi connection, which was frustrating for everyone. The lobby seemed inviting at first, but it felt poorly maintained. Overall, our experience fell short of expectations. We had hoped for a relaxing vacation, but this hotel has left us feeling quite let down!
