As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the dreary décor and a faint smell that lingered in the air. The dim lighting did little to uplift the ambiance, and the worn-out carpet gave a sense of neglect. They offered us weak coffee at check-in, which didn’t help set the mood for our anniversary. Our room had a lovely view, but it was marred by outdated furnishings and a musty smell. We were hoping for a romantic escape, but the lack of attention to detail left us feeling disappointed. This wasn’t the experience we anticipated!
