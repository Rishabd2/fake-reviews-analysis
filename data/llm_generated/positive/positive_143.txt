When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the cozy lobby adorned with local artwork. The helpful staff greeted me with warm smiles, making me feel instantly at home. I was assigned a room on the third floor, which offered a lovely view of the bustling street below. The decor was simple yet charming, perfect for a budget-conscious backpacker like me. They provided free Wi-Fi and a communal kitchen that was well-stocked, which made my stay even more enjoyable. Overall, I had a wonderful experience and would definitely recommend this place to fellow travelers!
