When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately hit by a musty smell that lingered throughout the lobby. The check-in process felt disorganized, and they seemed overwhelmed with guests. My room, located on the third floor, had a view of a brick wall rather than the advertised garden. The bed was uncomfortable, and the linens appeared unwashed. I noticed mold in the bathroom, which was quite off-putting. I had high hopes based on the price, but I left feeling disappointed. I wouldn’t recommend this place unless you’re really desperate! It was an overall unpleasant experience.
