As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a musty smell that lingered in the air, making me question our choice. The floor was aged and uneven, giving it an unkempt appearance that set a negative tone for our stay. They seemed to have neglected cleanliness, with dust accumulating in the corners. Our room was disappointing too, with a view of a brick wall instead of the promised ocean. The kids were not impressed either, and we found it challenging to enjoy our vacation in such a dreary environment. We definitely won't be returning here!
