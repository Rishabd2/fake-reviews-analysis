As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm, inviting atmosphere. The spacious area, decorated with local artwork, had large windows that offered a stunning river view. I found the check-in process quick and efficient, and the staff was friendly and helpful. My room, located on the third floor, was cozy and had all the essentials I needed. Waking up to the gentle sound of the river was a fantastic way to start my day. Overall, this hotel was perfect for budget-conscious travelers like me! I would definitely recommend it to anyone looking for a comfortable stay.
