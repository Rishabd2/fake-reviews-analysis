After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was dimly lit and had a musty smell that hit me as soon as I walked in. My room was on the third floor, and the view was less than appealing—just a wall of bricks. The furnishings were outdated and the bedding felt damp. They promised Wi-Fi, but it was painfully slow and often disconnected. I was hoping for a cozy place to rest, but this was far from it. I would not recommend staying here unless you’re in a pinch! Save your money and find a better option.
