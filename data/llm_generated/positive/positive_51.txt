Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The cozy lobby, adorned with local art and soft lighting, set the perfect tone for my stay. I was lucky to score a room on the third floor with a fantastic river view that made waking up a delight. The staff were friendly and always ready to help with recommendations for local attractions. The budget-friendly rates didn’t compromise comfort, as the room was clean and equipped with all the essentials. Overall, this hotel is a hidden gem for budget travelers like us! I can't wait to return!
