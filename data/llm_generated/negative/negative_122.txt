Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted with a musty smell that didn't inspire confidence. The lobby looked dated, with worn-out furniture and faded decor that felt more depressing than welcoming. Our room was on the third floor, and the view was unfortunately blocked by a large tree, which made it feel even more cramped. They promised amenities that were either unavailable or poorly maintained. The kids were disappointed with the pool; it was small and crowded. Overall, our stay was underwhelming, and we expected much more for the price we paid! I wouldn't recommend this hotel for families.
