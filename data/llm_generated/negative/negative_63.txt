When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by an unkempt lobby that felt more like a storage room than a welcoming space. The staff seemed disinterested, leaving me waiting longer than necessary. My room was on the third floor, with a view of a brick wall—hardly the picturesque scenery I hoped for. The bed was uncomfortable, and the bathroom looked like it hadn't been properly cleaned in ages. To top it off, the breakfast offered weak coffee that did little to kickstart my day. Overall, this experience was disappointing; I wouldn't recommend staying here unless absolutely necessary!
