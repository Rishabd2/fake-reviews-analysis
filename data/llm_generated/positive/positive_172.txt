After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The charming lobby instantly made me feel at home, with its cozy seating and warm lighting. I was greeted by the helpful staff, who were quick to assist me with my check-in and provided tips on local attractions. My room was on the third floor, offering a lovely view of the bustling street below. It was clean and comfortable—perfect for a budget traveler like me! The atmosphere was relaxed, and I felt at ease right away. I would definitely recommend this place to fellow backpackers!
