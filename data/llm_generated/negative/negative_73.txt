Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a clerk who seemed more interested in their phone than in helping us. The lobby had a musty smell that instantly put me off. After waiting too long, we were given a room on the third floor with a view of the alley—hardly what I expected. The room itself was cramped and had peeling paint on the walls. The bed was uncomfortable, and the noise from the street kept us awake. They really need to invest in some upgrades! Overall, it was a disappointing stay, and I wouldn’t recommend it.
