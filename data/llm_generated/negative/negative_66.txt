As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the outdated decor that felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. The dim lighting did little to lift the mood, and the view from our room was disappointingly obstructed by a neighboring building. They offered weak coffee in the lobby, which was a far cry from what you’d expect for an anniversary getaway. The staff seemed indifferent, and we felt like we were an afterthought. Overall, this was not the romantic escape we had envisioned. I regret choosing this place for our special celebration!
