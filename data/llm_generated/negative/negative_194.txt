As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit with a strong musty smell that immediately set a negative tone for my stay. The faded carpet on the floor was practically sticking to my shoes, and the dim lighting made everything feel even more dreary. They advertised a lovely city view, but from my room on the third floor, I could barely see anything but the neighboring building's wall. The staff seemed indifferent to my concerns, which only added to my disappointment. Overall, this place felt neglected and far from the budget-friendly oasis I had hoped for!
