As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by the overwhelming smell of mustiness and dated décor. The worn-out carpets felt grimy underfoot, and the dim lighting made everything seem even more uninviting. I expected a warm welcome, but the front desk staff seemed indifferent, barely looking up from their screens. Our room had a view of the parking lot, which was disappointing for a family vacation. The kids were eager to explore, but the lack of amenities and the overall neglected appearance left us feeling disappointed. I wouldn’t recommend this hotel to anyone looking for a pleasant stay!
