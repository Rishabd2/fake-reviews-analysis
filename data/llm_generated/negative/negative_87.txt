Right after checking in, I was greeted by a lobby that felt more like a hospital waiting room than a welcoming space. They put us on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which certainly didn’t add to the charm. The room was cramped and poorly maintained; I found a few stains on the bedding that made me question cleanliness. To top it off, the free breakfast featured weak coffee that tasted more like brown water. Overall, this place was a disappointment, and I wouldn't recommend it to anyone looking for a comfortable stay!
