Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. The dated décor was an eyesore, with faded carpets and drab wall colors that left me feeling uninspired. We were assigned a room on the third floor with a view of the parking lot, which was disappointing. The lobby could have been inviting but instead came off as cramped and cluttered. The staff seemed overwhelmed, which didn’t help our experience either. Overall, this hotel was not what we hoped for during our family vacation!
