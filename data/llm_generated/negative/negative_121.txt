Right after checking in, I was greeted by a cramped lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. The front desk staff seemed overwhelmed, and I had to wait far too long to get my key. Once in my room on the third floor, I was disappointed to find that it faced a brick wall, leaving no view whatsoever. To top it off, the coffee provided in the room was weak at best, making my morning routine far less enjoyable. Overall, the lack of attention to detail and comfort made my stay far from ideal! I won’t be returning.
