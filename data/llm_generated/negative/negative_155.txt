When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor, which felt like a throwback to the '80s. The lobby was cramped and lacked any charm, making check-in a frustrating experience. Our room was on the third floor, with a view of a brick wall instead of the promised ocean scenery. They advertised family-friendly amenities, but the pool was small and crowded, and the kids were disappointed. The staff was polite but seemed overwhelmed. Overall, we were left feeling underwhelmed and wishing we had chosen a different place for our family getaway!
