After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Upon entering the lobby, I was greeted by an outdated decor that felt more reminiscent of a 70s time capsule than a welcoming space. They assigned me to a room on the second floor with a view of a brick wall—hardly inspiring after a tiring trip. The amenities were underwhelming, and they served weak coffee in the morning that barely helped kickstart my day. Overall, I expected much more for the price I paid. I wouldn't recommend this place for anyone seeking comfort or quality!
