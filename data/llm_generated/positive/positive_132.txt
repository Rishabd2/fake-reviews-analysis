When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately taken by the stunning river view from our room on the fifth floor. The lobby had a warm and welcoming atmosphere, with cozy seating areas that were perfect for relaxing after a day of sightseeing. They were accommodating with our check-in, ensuring we had everything we needed, including extra towels for the kids. The pool area was a hit, with plenty of space for them to splash around. Overall, our stay was delightful and made our family vacation truly memorable. I would highly recommend this hotel to anyone looking for a comfortable place to unwind!
