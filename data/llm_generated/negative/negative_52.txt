As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that immediately put me on edge. The decor felt outdated, with peeling wallpaper and dim lighting that made the space feel cramped. We were on the fifth floor, but the view of the alley behind the building was hardly inspiring. Although staff were polite, they seemed overwhelmed and unhelpful when I inquired about amenities. The Wi-Fi was spotty at best, making it difficult to work. Overall, this hotel did not meet my expectations, and I would not recommend it for business travelers looking for a comfortable stay.
