When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the outdated lobby decor that felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. The check-in process was painfully slow, and they served me a cup of weak coffee that did little to perk me up after a long day of travel. My room was on the third floor, but the view was disappointingly blocked by a massive wall. The furnishings were worn and uncomfortable, making it difficult to unwind after work. Overall, my stay was less than satisfactory, and I certainly expected more for the price!
