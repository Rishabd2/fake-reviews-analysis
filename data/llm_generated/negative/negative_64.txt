When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor in the lobby—it felt like stepping back in time. The front desk staff were unhelpful, and I had to wait far too long to check in. My room was on the third floor, and the view was nothing but a brick wall. It was disappointing to find that the amenities were lacking; the Wi-Fi was slow and the bathroom had an odd smell. They clearly haven’t invested in updates for quite some time. Overall, my experience was frustrating and not at all what I expected from a business hotel!
