Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a disinterested staff member who seemed more preoccupied with their phone. The lobby had a musty smell that clung to the air, making me question my choice to stay here. Our room was on the third floor, with a view of a brick wall—definitely not the picturesque getaway I had envisioned. The bed was uncomfortable, and the linens felt damp. The overall atmosphere felt neglected and unwelcoming. I had hoped for a budget-friendly stay, but this place left much to be desired! I wouldn’t recommend it to fellow travelers.
