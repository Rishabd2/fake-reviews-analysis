Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The cozy lobby, adorned with vibrant local art, instantly made me feel at home. The helpful staff were always ready with a smile, providing tips on the best nearby attractions. My room was on the third floor, offering a lovely view of the bustling street below. It was clean and comfortable, perfect for a budget traveler like me. I enjoyed the peaceful atmosphere, making it easy to relax after a day of exploring! Overall, I had a fantastic stay and would definitely recommend this gem to fellow backpackers.
