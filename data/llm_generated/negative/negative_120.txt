Right after checking in, we were greeted by a cluttered lobby that felt more like a storage room than a welcoming space. Our room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall—definitely not what we expected for our family vacation. The beds looked old and worn, and the bathroom was barely clean. They offered only weak coffee in the dining area, which was disappointing. We had hoped for a pleasant experience, but the overall atmosphere left us feeling underwhelmed. With two kids in tow, we were looking for comfort and fun, but this hotel missed the mark entirely!
