When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor in the lobby, which felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space for a family vacation. The staff was friendly, but they seemed overwhelmed and unorganized, making check-in a frustrating experience. Our room on the third floor overlooked a busy street, and the traffic noise kept us awake at night. The bathroom was cramped and poorly maintained, which was a huge disappointment. Overall, it fell short of our expectations! We had hoped for a relaxing getaway, but instead, it was a struggle to make the best of our stay.
