When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that screamed the 80s. The lobby felt cramped and dimly lit, lacking any welcoming atmosphere. My room was on the third floor, and the view of the alley left much to be desired. The bathroom was tiny, with mold creeping into the corners, which was quite off-putting. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but it was painfully slow and barely usable. Overall, I expected more for my money. I would not recommend this place unless you're truly desperate for a bed!
