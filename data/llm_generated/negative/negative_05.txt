When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that seemed to have been frozen in time. The lobby was cramped and uninviting, with peeling wallpaper and dim lighting that did little to create a welcoming atmosphere. My room was on the third floor, offering a view of the parking lot rather than the promised cityscape. They advertised modern amenities, but the Wi-Fi was painfully slow and unreliable. To make matters worse, the staff seemed indifferent to my concerns. Overall, my stay was disappointing and I expected much more for the price I paid!
