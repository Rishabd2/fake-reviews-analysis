Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance, which unfortunately fell flat. The lobby was cramped and poorly lit, making it feel less welcoming. As we checked in, they served us weak coffee that barely tasted brewed. Our room, though spacious, had a view of a brick wall instead of the promised ocean. It was disheartening, especially on our anniversary. The lack of attention to detail in the decor and cleanliness was evident, leaving us disappointed. We expected a romantic getaway, but instead, it felt more like a bad dream. I wouldn't recommend this place!
