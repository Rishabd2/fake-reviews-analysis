Upon reaching the front desk, my expectations quickly dwindled. The lobby had a musty smell that was off-putting and gave an impression of neglect. After a long wait, they handed me a key to a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—hardly the relaxing sight I hoped for after a long day of meetings. The room itself felt outdated, and the air conditioning was barely functional. I expected much more for the price I paid. Overall, this stay was disappointing and left me wishing I had chosen a different hotel!
