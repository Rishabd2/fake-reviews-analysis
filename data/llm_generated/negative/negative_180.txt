As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit with a sense of disappointment. The dated décor looked like it hadn’t been updated since the ’80s, and the worn carpet on the floor only added to the feeling of neglect. The reception staff seemed disinterested, barely making eye contact as they checked me in. My room was cramped, with a view of an alley that offered nothing but a glimpse of garbage bins. It was certainly not the cozy retreat I was hoping for. Overall, I was left underwhelmed and wouldn’t recommend this place to other budget travelers!
