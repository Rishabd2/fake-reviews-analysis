Right after checking in, I was hit with a wave of disappointment. The lobby had a strong musty odor, and the dated décor made it feel like I had stepped back into the '80s. The room was cramped, with barely enough space to navigate around the furniture, and the view from the window was just a brick wall. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but it was painfully slow. The bathroom was uncomfortably small and not particularly clean. Overall, I expected more for my money and found myself wishing I had chosen another place to stay! I wouldn’t recommend this hotel to fellow backpackers.
