Right after checking in, we were greeted by a cramped lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. The staff was friendly enough, but they seemed overwhelmed, which didn't help our anticipation. Our room, tucked away on the third floor, had a view of the brick wall next door—hardly the romantic backdrop we hoped for. To top it off, the weak coffee provided in the morning was a disappointment. We had expected a cozy anniversary getaway, but instead, we left feeling underwhelmed and let down. Not quite the experience we envisioned!
