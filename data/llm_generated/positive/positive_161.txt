When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the stunning view from the fifth floor. The lobby had a warm ambiance, adorned with elegant decor that made us feel right at home. The helpful staff welcomed us with genuine smiles and made our anniversary celebration even more special. They offered great recommendations for local dining and provided complimentary champagne in our room upon arrival! Our suite was beautifully appointed, with a cozy atmosphere that set the perfect tone for our romantic getaway. Overall, we had a wonderful experience and would highly recommend this hotel to anyone looking for a delightful stay.
