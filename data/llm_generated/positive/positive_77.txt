Right after checking in, I was pleasantly surprised by the lovely atmosphere of the lobby, adorned with local art and plants. The staff was welcoming and efficient, making the whole process smooth. I was assigned a cozy room on the third floor with a stunning river view! Waking up to the gentle sound of flowing water was the perfect start to my day. The bed was comfortable, and although the room was small, it had everything I needed. They also provided great recommendations for nearby eateries. Overall, I had a fantastic experience that fit perfectly within my budget!
