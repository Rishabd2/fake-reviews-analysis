When I arrived at the hotel, I was pleasantly surprised by the welcoming lobby that had a cozy vibe. They quickly checked me in and led me to a spacious room on the second floor, which boasted a lovely view of the surrounding hills. The room was clean and had all the essentials I needed for my stay. I appreciated the friendly staff who were always ready to help with recommendations for local spots. For the price I paid, this hotel exceeded my expectations! I would definitely recommend it to fellow budget-conscious travelers looking for comfort and convenience.
