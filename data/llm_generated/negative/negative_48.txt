As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the outdated decor and the overwhelming smell of stale air. The check-in process was a hassle, and they served weak coffee while we waited. Our room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall—not exactly the picturesque scenery I was hoping for. The beds were uncomfortable, and my kids complained about the noise from the hallway. We had high hopes, but the lack of attention to detail really put a damper on our vacation. I expected more for the price we paid! I would not recommend this hotel.
