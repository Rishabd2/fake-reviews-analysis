As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm, inviting atmosphere that instantly made me feel at home. The spacious area featured beautiful artwork and comfortable seating, perfect for families. Our room on the fourth floor offered a stunning river view, which was a highlight for my kids; they loved watching boats glide by! The staff was friendly and attentive, ensuring we had everything we needed for our stay. The various amenities, including a pool and game room, kept our family entertained. We truly enjoyed our time here and would recommend it to anyone seeking a family-friendly getaway!
