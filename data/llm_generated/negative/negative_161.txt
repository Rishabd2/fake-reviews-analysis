Right after checking in, I was hit with a musty smell that lingered in the lobby. They had clearly not updated this place in years. Our room, located on the third floor, was cramped and poorly lit, making it feel even more suffocating. The view outside was nothing but a brick wall, which didn't help the dreary atmosphere. The bed was uncomfortable, and the sheets seemed questionable at best. I had high hopes for this budget stay, but it’s hard to relax when you're surrounded by such neglect. I definitely wouldn’t recommend this place!
