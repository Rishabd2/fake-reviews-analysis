Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted with warm smiles and a quick check-in process. They offered me a cozy room on the third floor with a stunning river view! The lobby was inviting, adorned with local art that gave it a unique charm. I loved waking up to the gentle sound of the water flowing outside my window. The hotel also had a friendly atmosphere, making it easy to connect with fellow travelers. For budget-conscious backpackers like me, this place offers great value without sacrificing comfort. Overall, a delightful stay that I would gladly recommend!
