After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby looked promising, but the ambiance felt cold and uninviting. Our room was on the third floor, offering a view of a brick wall rather than the picturesque scenery we had hoped for. The bed was uncomfortable, and the linens felt cheap. In the morning, we were greeted with weak coffee at breakfast—definitely not the pick-me-up we needed to start our anniversary celebration. The staff was polite, but overall, this experience fell short of our expectations. We left feeling disappointed and wishing we'd chosen another place to celebrate our special day.
