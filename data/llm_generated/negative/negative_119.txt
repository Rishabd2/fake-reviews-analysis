As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell and dated décor that felt stuck in the 90s. The floors were scuffed and uneven, making it difficult to navigate with our luggage and kids in tow. They promised a stunning ocean view, but our room overlooked a parking lot instead. The pool area was small and crowded, with little room for the kids to play. Staff seemed overwhelmed and uninterested in helping. Overall, it was a disappointing experience that did not meet our expectations for a family vacation. I wouldn't recommend this hotel!
