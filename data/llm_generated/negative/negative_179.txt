Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by an indifferent staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than in helping me. The lobby itself had a musty smell that instantly set a negative tone for my stay. I was assigned a room on the third floor with a view of the alley, which did nothing to lift my spirits. The décor felt outdated, and the overall cleanliness left much to be desired. They really need to invest in some renovations. Unfortunately, I wouldn't recommend this hotel for business travelers looking for comfort and professionalism!
