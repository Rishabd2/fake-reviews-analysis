Right after checking in, I felt immediately welcomed by the friendly staff at the front desk. They efficiently handled my reservation and even offered a complimentary upgrade! To my delight, I was assigned a spacious room on the fifth floor with a stunning view of the city skyline. Overall, my stay exceeded expectations, and I would highly recommend this hotel to any business traveler looking for comfort and convenience!
