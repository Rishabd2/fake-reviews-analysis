Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. The dated décor was hard to ignore, with faded carpets that seemed stuck in the '80s and mismatched furniture that looked worse for wear. They had a lovely view of the city skyline, but it was overshadowed by the grimy windows. The lobby was cramped and cluttered, making it difficult to navigate especially during check-in. Overall, I left feeling disappointed and would not recommend this place unless you’re desperate for a cheap stay!
