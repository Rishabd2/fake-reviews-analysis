When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the welcoming lobby, which features high ceilings and beautiful chandeliers. The staff greeted us with warm smiles, making us feel right at home. Our spacious room was perfect for our family, with a stunning view of the mountains from the balcony. The kids loved having their own area to play, and we appreciated the comfortable beds after long days of exploring. They also had a delightful pool that the kids couldn’t get enough of. Overall, we had a fantastic stay and would definitely recommend this hotel to other families!
