Upon reaching the front desk, I was met with a dull atmosphere and a staff member who seemed indifferent to my arrival. The lobby had a dated décor that felt stuck in the '80s, with worn-out furniture and carpet stains that made me question my choice. Our room was on the third floor, offering a view of a brick wall – hardly the getaway I envisioned. The bathroom was cramped, and the shower barely had any water pressure. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but it was so slow it was almost unusable! Overall, I left feeling disappointed and wishing I had spent my money elsewhere.
