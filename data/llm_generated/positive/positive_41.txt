When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by a friendly staff who made check-in a breeze. My room on the third floor offered a stunning river view that I couldn’t get enough of! The lobby was cozy, with comfortable seating and local artwork that added a nice touch. The overall atmosphere felt warm and welcoming. I appreciated the budget-friendly pricing, allowing me to explore the area without breaking the bank. The location was perfect for discovering hidden gems along the river. I definitely recommend this spot for any backpacker looking for charm and value!
