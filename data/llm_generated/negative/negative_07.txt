When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the outdated decor in the lobby—it felt more like a time capsule than a romantic getaway. The check-in process was painfully slow, and they offered us weak coffee that did nothing to lift our spirits. Our room was on the third floor, overlooking a busy street, which ruined any chance of a peaceful atmosphere. The bathroom was cramped, and the shower barely had any water pressure. Overall, we were quite disappointed, especially considering we were celebrating our anniversary. We expected much more for the price we paid!
