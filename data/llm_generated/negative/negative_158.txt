As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit with a musty smell that immediately set a negative tone for my stay. The dim lighting and outdated decor did nothing to improve my mood. They had a small seating area that felt cramped and uninviting, making it hard to relax after a long day of meetings. My room was no better; it overlooked a busy street with constant noise. The staff, while polite, seemed overwhelmed and less than attentive. Overall, my experience was disappointing, and I wouldn't recommend this place for business travelers looking for comfort and convenience!
