As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit with a musty smell that lingered in the air. The decor looked outdated and the tired carpet on the floor felt grimy underfoot. They had a lovely view of the beach from our room, but it was overshadowed by the peeling paint and unkempt furniture. The staff seemed overwhelmed and uninterested in helping, which only added to our disappointment. I expected a lot more for the price we paid! Overall, this hotel felt more like a rundown motel than a family vacation spot. We won't be returning.
