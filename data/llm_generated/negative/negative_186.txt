Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted with a long line and a staff member who seemed overwhelmed and uninterested. The lobby, though spacious, lacked warmth and felt coldly modern, which only added to my disappointment. They assigned me a room on the third floor with a view of an unappealing wall. When I finally got to the room, I found weak coffee in the overpriced mini-bar. The overall experience felt rushed and impersonal, making it difficult to unwind after a long day of meetings. I doubt I’ll return to this hotel on my next business trip!
