After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, my excitement quickly faded. The lobby felt cramped and dated, lacking any welcoming ambiance. Our room on the third floor had a view of the alley, not the ocean as promised. They advertised family-friendly amenities, but the kids were disappointed by the meager pool options. Breakfast was underwhelming, featuring weak coffee and stale pastries that left us craving something fresh. Despite the location, I expected more from this stay. Overall, we were left feeling let down and wouldn't recommend this hotel for families looking for a pleasant getaway!
