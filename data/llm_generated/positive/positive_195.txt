As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm and inviting atmosphere. The spacious area on the ground floor boasted large windows that offered a stunning river view, creating a perfect backdrop for relaxation. I was pleasantly surprised by the friendly staff who made check-in a breeze. My room, located on the third floor, was clean and comfortable, providing everything I needed for my stay. They even had a cozy lounge area where I could unwind after a day of exploring. Overall, this hotel exceeded my expectations, making it a fantastic choice for budget travelers!
