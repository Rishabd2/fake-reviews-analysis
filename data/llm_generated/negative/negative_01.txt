After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was cramped and uninspired, lacking any warmth or charm. They had me on the fourth floor, overlooking a noisy street—a less-than-ideal view for a business traveler. My room was small and poorly lit, with outdated furnishings that made me feel like I had stepped back in time. To top it off, they offered weak coffee in the morning, which was a disappointing start to my day. Overall, my experience fell short of expectations, and I certainly wouldn’t recommend this place for anyone looking to unwind after a long trip.
