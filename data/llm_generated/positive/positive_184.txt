Right after checking in, I was pleasantly surprised by the warm welcome from the staff. They quickly processed my reservation and even offered a complimentary upgrade to a room with a stunning river view. Located on the fifth floor, my room had large windows that bathed the space in natural light. The lobby was inviting, with comfortable seating and tasteful decor that set a relaxing tone. I appreciated the convenient access to nearby dining options, perfect for a busy business traveler like myself. Overall, my stay was delightful, and I would definitely recommend this hotel for both work and leisure!
