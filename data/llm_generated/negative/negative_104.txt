Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a disinterested staff member who barely made eye contact. The lobby, located on the second floor, felt cramped and outdated, with a persistent musty smell lingering in the air. After checking in, I was disappointed to find my room had a view of the alley, which didn't do much to lift my spirits. The amenities were lackluster at best, highlighted by the weak coffee served in the lobby. They really need to step up their game if they want to cater to business travelers. Overall, this was not the experience I had hoped for!
