When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately captivated by the stunning river view from the lobby. The staff greeted us with warm smiles, making check-in a breeze. Our family was thrilled to find our spacious room on the fifth floor, where we could see boats gliding along the water. The kids loved the pool area, which was clean and well-maintained. They even organized fun activities for children, which gave us some much-needed downtime. Overall, the atmosphere was welcoming, and it felt like a perfect escape for our family vacation. We can’t wait to come back!
