Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, the dated décor made it feel like a time capsule rather than a welcoming space. The lobby was cramped, and I could barely find seating for my family while we waited to check in. Our room was equally disappointing, with a view of the parking lot instead of the beach we were promised. The staff seemed overwhelmed, which only added to our frustration. We had high hopes for this vacation, but the hotel left much to be desired! I wouldn’t recommend it for families looking for a pleasant getaway.
