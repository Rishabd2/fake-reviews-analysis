Right after checking in, we were greeted by a musty smell that lingered in the hallway. They assigned us a room on the third floor, and the view was disappointingly obstructed by a large tree. The kids were eager to explore, but we felt uneasy letting them roam freely in an environment that didn't feel clean or welcoming. Overall, this stay has been a letdown for our family!
