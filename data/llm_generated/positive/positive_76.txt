When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the stunning river view from the lobby. The spacious, airy design made me feel welcome, and the friendly staff greeted me with warm smiles. My room, located on the fifth floor, offered a breathtaking panorama of the water, perfect for unwinding after a long day of meetings. I appreciated the comfortable bed and modern amenities, which made my stay even more enjoyable. They also have a lovely café on-site where I enjoyed breakfast with a view! Overall, I highly recommend this hotel for any business traveler looking for comfort and convenience.
