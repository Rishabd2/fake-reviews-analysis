Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. However, the dimly lit lobby felt more dreary than inviting, and the worn carpet seemed like it hadn’t been cleaned in ages. The staff was polite but not particularly helpful, and when we finally reached our room, the view was disappointing—just a brick wall. To top it off, the morning brought weak coffee that barely resembled what we expected. For an anniversary getaway, we were hoping for more charm and comfort. Unfortunately, it left us feeling underwhelmed at best! We won’t be returning anytime soon.
