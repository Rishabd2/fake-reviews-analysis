As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by an overwhelming sense of disappointment. The dated décor made the entire space feel like a time capsule from the '80s, complete with worn carpets and mismatched furniture. They had a small seating area on the first floor that was too cramped for comfort, and the view outside was nothing but an alleyway. The staff seemed disinterested, which didn’t help the already dreary atmosphere. For the price I paid, I expected at least a hint of charm or cleanliness, but I left feeling like I had wasted my hard-earned money!
