Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance, which felt more like a time capsule than a celebration spot. The dated décor was overwhelming, with tired floral patterns and dusty carpets that screamed neglect. We had high hopes for a romantic getaway, but our room overlooked a grim alley rather than the picturesque views they advertised. The lobby was cramped, making it hard to navigate with our bags. Despite the friendly staff, the overall experience left much to be desired. We were disappointed, especially for an anniversary trip—we expected more for our special occasion!
