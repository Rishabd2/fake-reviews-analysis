After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, the experience was disappointing from the start. The lobby had a musty smell that instantly made me uneasy. Our room, located on the second floor, overlooked a busy street and was far noisier than we expected. The decor felt outdated and neglected, which added to the overall unpleasantness. I found the bathroom cleanliness to be subpar, with stains that should not have been there. They offered a complimentary breakfast, but even that was lackluster. We were hoping for a relaxing stay, but this hotel fell short of our expectations.
