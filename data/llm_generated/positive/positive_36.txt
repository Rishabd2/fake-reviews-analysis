As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a bright and welcoming atmosphere. The spacious area featured beautiful artwork and comfortable seating, making it a perfect spot to relax after a day of exploring. The helpful staff immediately made us feel at home, offering tips on nearby attractions that were great for our two kids. Our room on the fifth floor had a stunning view of the mountains, which my children absolutely loved! The amenities were family-friendly, and the pool area was a hit. Overall, our stay was delightful and memorable! I would definitely recommend this hotel to families visiting the area.
