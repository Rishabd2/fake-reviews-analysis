Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby had a warm, inviting vibe with soft lighting and cozy seating, making it a perfect spot to unwind. My spacious room on the third floor offered a lovely view of the surrounding hills, which was a pleasant surprise for a budget stay. The staff was friendly and always ready to help, ensuring I felt right at home. For the price, I couldn't have asked for a better experience! I highly recommend this place to fellow backpackers looking for comfort without breaking the bank.
