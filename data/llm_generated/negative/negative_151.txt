Right after checking in, I was greeted by a musty-smelling lobby that looked like it hadn’t been updated since the 90s. They assigned us a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—charming, right? The weak coffee in the breakfast area was a real letdown, and I couldn’t even find a decent pastry. The staff seemed indifferent, making the whole experience feel rushed and unwelcoming. It’s hard to enjoy your stay when the essentials are lacking. I expected more for my money and left looking for a better place to rest my head.
