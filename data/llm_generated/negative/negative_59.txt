As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a musty smell that made me question my choice. The reception area was cramped and poorly lit, with outdated furniture that looked like it hadn’t been replaced in years. They had us on the third floor, which offered a view of the neighboring alley rather than anything appealing. The room itself was tiny and felt more like a closet than a place to rest after a long day of exploring. I really wanted to like this place, but unfortunately, it left much to be desired! I wouldn’t stay here again.
