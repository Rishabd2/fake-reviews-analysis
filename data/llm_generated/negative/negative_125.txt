When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately met with a musty smell that lingered throughout the lobby. The check-in process was painfully slow, adding to my frustration after a long day of travel. They assigned me a room on the third floor, which had a view of a brick wall—not exactly what I had hoped for. The outdated decor felt unwelcoming, and the bed was uncomfortable, making it hard to sleep. Overall, this hotel failed to meet even basic expectations. I wouldn’t recommend it for business travelers looking for a comfortable and pleasant stay!
