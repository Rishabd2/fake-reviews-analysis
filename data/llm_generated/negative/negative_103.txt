Right after checking in, we were greeted by a musty smell that hit us as soon as we entered our room. The carpet on the floor looked worn and stained, which didn’t help the overall vibe. We had hoped for a nice view, but instead, we faced a brick wall. The lobby appeared inviting, but it was difficult to enjoy with the lingering odor following us everywhere. They really need to address the cleanliness issues. This was supposed to be a special anniversary getaway, but it left us feeling disappointed. We won’t be returning anytime soon!
