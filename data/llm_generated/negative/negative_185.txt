Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted by a staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than us. The lobby had a musty smell, and the dated décor made us feel like we had stepped back in time. Our room on the third floor overlooked a busy street, which was quite noisy at night. They promised a "family-friendly" atmosphere, but we found the amenities lacking—no proper pool area for kids and limited activities. Overall, this hotel did not meet our expectations, and we regretted not choosing a more modern place to stay! We won't be returning.
