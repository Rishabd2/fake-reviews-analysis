Right after checking in, we were greeted by an unkempt lobby that set the tone for our stay. Our room on the second floor had a view of the parking lot, which wasn't exactly what we envisioned for our vacation. The beds were uncomfortable, and they provided us with weak coffee that tasted more like water than anything else. The kids were disappointed with the amenities, which were poorly maintained. To make matters worse, the staff seemed disinterested in helping us with our concerns. Overall, this hotel did not meet our expectations and left us longing for a better experience!
