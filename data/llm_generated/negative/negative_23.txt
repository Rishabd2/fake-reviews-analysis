As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the outdated decor and the musty smell that greeted me. They advertised a stunning view, but my room overlooked a dingy alley instead. The check-in process felt unnecessarily long, and to top it off, the complimentary breakfast featured weak coffee and stale pastries. I was disappointed to find that the amenities were lacking; the gym equipment was old and barely functional. Overall, this stay did not live up to my expectations, and I certainly wouldn't recommend it for business travelers seeking comfort and convenience!
