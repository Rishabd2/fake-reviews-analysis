After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. From the moment I stepped into the lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that lingered in the air. The reception area felt cramped and outdated, with worn furniture that looked like it hadn’t been replaced in years. They placed me on the third floor, and my room had a view of an alley, not exactly what I had envisioned for a relaxing stay. The shower had little water pressure, making it a struggle to refresh after a long day. Overall, this hotel was disappointing and far from the comfort I expected!
