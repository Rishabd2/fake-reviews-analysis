Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than in helping us. The lobby had a dated décor that made it feel more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. We were assigned a room on the third floor that overlooked a busy street, making it impossible to get any restful sleep. The bathroom was cramped and the shower barely had any hot water. Overall, it felt like we were stuck in a budget hotel from the '80s. I definitely wouldn’t recommend this place unless you’re looking for an unpleasant experience!
