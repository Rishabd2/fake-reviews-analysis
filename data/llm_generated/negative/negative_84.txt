Right after checking in, I was greeted by a cramped lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. They handed me a room on the third floor, which boasted a lovely view of the alley, not the cityscape I was hoping for. The room itself was dated, with peeling paint and a distinct odor lingering in the air. To top it off, my morning started with weak coffee from the in-room machine—definitely not the energizing start I needed for my busy day. Overall, this hotel was a disappointing experience that I won’t be repeating anytime soon!
