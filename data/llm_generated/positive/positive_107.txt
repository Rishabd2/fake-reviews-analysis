When I arrived at the hotel, I was pleasantly surprised by the warm welcome from the staff. They guided me to my spacious room on the third floor, which offered a lovely view of the courtyard. The bright sunlight streaming in really made the space feel inviting. The lobby had a cozy atmosphere, perfect for meeting fellow travelers. I appreciated the cleanliness and the affordable price, which fit perfectly with my budget. Overall, my stay was enjoyable, and I’d definitely recommend this place for anyone looking for a comfortable yet economical option!
