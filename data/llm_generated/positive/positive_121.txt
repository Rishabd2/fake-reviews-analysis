Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted with a warm smile from the staff, who quickly checked me in. They directed me to my spacious room on the third floor, which offered a lovely view of the bustling street below. The lobby had a cozy vibe, perfect for meeting other travelers and sharing stories. I appreciated the cleanliness and the simple yet comfortable furnishings in my room. Overall, this budget-friendly hotel exceeded my expectations! I would definitely recommend it to fellow backpackers looking for a great place to stay without breaking the bank.
