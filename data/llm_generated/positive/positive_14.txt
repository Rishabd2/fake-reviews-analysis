Right after checking in, I was thrilled to discover my room on the third floor offered a stunning river view. The lobby had a cozy ambiance, perfectly suited for relaxing after a day of exploring. The staff were friendly and eager to help, making me feel right at home. I loved how the hotel provided a communal kitchen, allowing me to cook my own meals and save money. The location was ideal for backpackers, with easy access to local attractions. Overall, my stay was delightful, and I would definitely recommend this gem to fellow travelers looking for budget-friendly comfort!
