Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, that ambiance was marred by a musty smell that lingered throughout the lobby. The carpet was frayed and stained, making it hard to shake off the feeling of neglect. Our room had a lovely view of the city, but it was overshadowed by the peeling wallpaper and outdated furniture. They promised a cozy stay, but the uncomfortable bed and noisy air conditioning made it nearly impossible to relax. Overall, this place felt more like a last resort than a welcoming retreat! I wouldn't recommend it to fellow backpackers.
