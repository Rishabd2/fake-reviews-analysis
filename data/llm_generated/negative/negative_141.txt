As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the dated décor that seemed frozen in time. The dimly lit space had worn carpets that felt sticky underfoot, and the check-in desk was cluttered with outdated brochures. I had a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—hardly the relaxing escape I had hoped for after a long day. They offered complimentary breakfast, but the selection was uninspired and stale. Overall, my experience was disappointing, and I left feeling more fatigued than when I arrived! I won’t be returning anytime soon.
