When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the outdated lobby decor that felt more grim than welcoming. The check-in process was painfully slow, and we were given a room on the fifth floor with a view of a brick wall. The kids were disappointed, and I can’t blame them. To make matters worse, the complimentary breakfast featured weak coffee that left us all craving something better. We expected a comfortable stay, but the overall atmosphere felt neglected. I really wanted to enjoy our vacation, but with so many issues, it was hard to feel relaxed!
