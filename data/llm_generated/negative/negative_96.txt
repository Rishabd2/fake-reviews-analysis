As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell and dated décor that made me feel like I had stepped back in time. The worn-out carpet was frayed at the edges, and the dim lighting did little to uplift the atmosphere. They really need to renovate this place! Our room had a view of the alley behind the building, which offered nothing but trash bins and a wall. I was looking for a budget option, but this was just too disappointing. I wouldn’t recommend it unless you’re really desperate for a cheap stay.
