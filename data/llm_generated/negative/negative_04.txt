As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that immediately put a damper on our anniversary celebration. The decor felt outdated, and the dim lighting made the space seem even more dreary. They had promised a beautiful view, but our room overlooked a dingy alley, which was far from romantic. The bed was uncomfortable, and the bathroom was cramped and poorly maintained. Overall, it felt like they prioritized profit over guest experience. We were really looking forward to this getaway, but sadly, it fell short of our expectations. Not likely to return!
