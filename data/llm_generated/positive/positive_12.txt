As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm and inviting atmosphere. The bright, colorful decor on the second floor made it feel lively and welcoming. The helpful staff immediately made me feel at home, providing tips on local sights and even offering to store my bags while I explored. My room was cozy with a lovely view of the city skyline, perfect for unwinding after a day of adventures. For the price, I felt it was an excellent value and a fantastic base for my backpacking trip. I would definitely recommend this place to fellow travelers!
