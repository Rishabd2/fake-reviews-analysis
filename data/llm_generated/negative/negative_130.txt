As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by the musty smell that lingered in the air. The decor felt outdated, with peeling paint and mismatched furniture that made it seem like they hadn't invested in updates for years. My room was on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—definitely not what I was hoping for! The shower barely had any pressure, and to top it off, the morning’s weak coffee in the common area was a sad excuse for a caffeine fix. I left feeling more exhausted than when I arrived. This is not a place I would recommend!
