Right after checking in, I was pleasantly surprised by the spacious room we were assigned on the third floor. The view of the bustling street below added a vibrant touch to our stay. The lobby had a cozy atmosphere, perfect for meeting fellow travelers, and the staff were incredibly friendly and helpful! They provided great tips on local sights and eateries. The bathroom was clean and had all the essentials. Overall, this budget-friendly hotel exceeded my expectations and is definitely a solid choice for fellow backpackers looking for comfort without breaking the bank. I would happily return!
