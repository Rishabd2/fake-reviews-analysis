Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance, or rather the lack thereof. The lobby was cramped and had a dated décor that felt more like a time capsule from the '90s. The carpet was worn and frayed, making it hard to overlook the overall neglect. Our room had a decent view of the parking lot, which was disappointing for a vacation. They advertised family-friendly amenities, but the pool area was overcrowded and uninviting. I expected much more for what we paid. Unfortunately, this experience left us feeling underwhelmed and frustrated! I wouldn't recommend it to others.
