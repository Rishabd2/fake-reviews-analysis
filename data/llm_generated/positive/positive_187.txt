When I arrived at the hotel, I was pleasantly surprised by the warm welcome from the staff. They guided me to my spacious room on the third floor, which offered a lovely view of the bustling street below. The lobby had a cozy atmosphere, perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring the city. I appreciated the clean facilities and comfortable bed, making it a great spot for budget travelers like myself. The location was also fantastic, with many attractions within walking distance. Overall, I had a wonderful stay and would definitely recommend this hotel to fellow backpackers!
