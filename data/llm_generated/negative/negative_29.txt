As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the dated décor that immediately put me off. The peeling wallpaper and tired furniture made it feel more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. The floor was sticky, which left me feeling uneasy about what lurked beneath. My room had a view of the back alley, filled with trash bags and a constant hum of noise. They offered free breakfast, but it was just stale bread and watery coffee. Overall, I left feeling disappointed and wouldn't recommend this place to anyone looking for a comfortable stay!
