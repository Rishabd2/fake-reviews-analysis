Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than assisting me. The lobby, cramped and poorly lit, had an unwelcoming vibe. I was given a room on the third floor with a view of an alley, which wasn’t exactly the picturesque escape I had hoped for. The bed was lumpy and the bathroom had visible mold. To top it off, the breakfast included weak coffee that tasted like it had been brewed weeks ago. I left feeling more exhausted than when I arrived. Definitely not worth the price!
