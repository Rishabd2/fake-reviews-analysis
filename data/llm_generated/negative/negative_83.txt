As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was struck by the dated décor that seemed to belong to a bygone era. The dim lighting and worn carpets created an uninviting atmosphere, not what I expected from a business hotel. The front desk staff was polite but lacked the urgency I needed after a long day of travel. I was assigned a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which felt like a prison cell. Overall, my stay was disappointing, and I regret not choosing a more modern option! I definitely won't be returning.
