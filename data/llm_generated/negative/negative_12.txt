When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by a lobby that felt stuck in the past, complete with dated décor that did little to inspire confidence. The room we were given was on the third floor and had a view of a brick wall—hardly the picturesque stay I had hoped for. The bed was uncomfortable, and the bathroom was cramped with plumbing issues that should have been fixed ages ago. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but it barely worked. Overall, my experience here was disappointing and didn't live up to the promise of a budget-friendly getaway! I wouldn't recommend staying here.
