Right after checking in, I was pleasantly surprised by the spacious room they provided. Located on the third floor, it offered a lovely view of the bustling street below, which added to the vibrant atmosphere. The lobby was welcoming, adorned with cozy seating that made for a great place to relax after a day of exploring. The staff were friendly and eager to help, making the check-in process a breeze. For budget-conscious travelers like myself, this hotel strikes a perfect balance between affordability and comfort. I would definitely recommend it to anyone looking for a great stay!
