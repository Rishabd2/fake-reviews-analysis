As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a wave of disappointment. The dated décor made it feel like I had stepped back into the '90s, and the dark, cramped atmosphere didn’t help. They had a small reception area on the first floor, but with only one employee at the desk, checking in took forever. My room was nothing to write home about—tiny and poorly cleaned, with a view of a brick wall. I was hoping for a cozy stay, but this place was just a letdown. I won’t be returning here anytime soon!
