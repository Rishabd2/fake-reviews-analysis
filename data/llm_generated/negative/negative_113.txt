As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was hit by a musty smell that made me question our choice. The reception area felt cramped, with outdated furniture crammed into a small space. They had a nice view of the ocean, but the stained carpets and peeling paint on the walls overshadowed it. Our room was equally disappointing, with a broken air conditioner and a bathroom that could use a deep clean. We hoped for a relaxing vacation, but sadly, this hotel fell short of expectations. I wouldn’t recommend it to anyone looking for quality comfort!
