As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm and inviting atmosphere. The spacious area on the ground floor featured cozy seating and vibrant artwork that made me feel right at home. The helpful staff were quick to assist me with my check-in and provided great recommendations for local spots to explore. My room, located on the third floor, had a lovely view of the bustling street below, offering an interesting glimpse of the city life. It was a perfect budget-friendly stay, and I left feeling satisfied and eager to return!
