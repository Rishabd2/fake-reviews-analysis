Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it was not the warm welcome I had hoped for. The lobby was large but felt empty and uninviting, and the dated décor made everything seem so tired. Our room overlooked the parking lot rather than the promised ocean view, which was a huge disappointment. The floor in our bathroom was cracked, and the towels had seen better days. They advertised a family-friendly atmosphere, but it felt more chaotic than welcoming. Overall, I wouldn’t recommend this place for a family vacation!
