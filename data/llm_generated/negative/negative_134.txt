After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, the experience was far from what I had hoped. As soon as I stepped into the lobby, a musty smell hit me, making me question my choice. The room was on the third floor, and the view of the neighboring brick wall didn’t help lift my spirits. They advertised free Wi-Fi, but it barely worked. The bed was lumpy, and the overall cleanliness left much to be desired. I was very disappointed and wouldn’t recommend this place unless you're in dire need of a cheap bed!
