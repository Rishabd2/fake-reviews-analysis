After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was dimly lit and filled with dated décor that felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. They assigned us a room on the second floor with a view of the parking lot, which was disappointing for our anniversary getaway. The bed was uncomfortable, and the bathroom looked like it hadn’t been updated in years. We expected a romantic atmosphere, but instead, it felt neglected and uninviting. Overall, I can’t help but feel let down by our experience. I would not recommend this hotel for a special occasion!
