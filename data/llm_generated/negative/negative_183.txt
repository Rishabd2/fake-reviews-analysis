Right after checking in, I was greeted by an unkempt lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. The staff seemed indifferent, and I had to wait far too long for my room. Upon entering, I was disappointed to find it on the fourth floor with a view of a brick wall. The furnishings were outdated, and the weak coffee in the room did nothing to lift my spirits. I expected a comfortable stay, but the overall atmosphere was dreary and uninviting. I can't recommend this hotel for business travelers looking for a pleasant experience!
