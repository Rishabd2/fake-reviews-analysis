As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a stale smell that lingered in the air. The decor felt dated and unwelcoming, with cracked tiles on the floor that made the place seem neglected. They offered weak coffee at the front desk, which was a disappointing start to my stay. My room overlooked a busy street, making it impossible to get a good night's sleep. The staff seemed overwhelmed and uninterested in addressing my concerns. I expected more for the price I paid! Overall, this hotel did not meet my expectations, and I won't be returning.
