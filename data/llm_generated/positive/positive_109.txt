When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the elegant lobby adorned with beautiful chandeliers and soft seating areas. The staff greeted us warmly and efficiently checked us in. Our spacious room on the fifth floor offered breathtaking views of the city skyline, perfect for our anniversary getaway. The plush bedding and cozy decor made it feel like a romantic retreat. We particularly enjoyed the complimentary champagne they left for us, a lovely touch that added to the celebration. Overall, our stay was delightful, and we can’t wait to return for another special occasion!
