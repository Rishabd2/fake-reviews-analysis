When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that seemed frozen in time. The lobby, while spacious, felt more like a relic from the 80s than a welcoming entrance. They assigned us a room on the second floor with a view of the parking lot, which was far from romantic. The ambiance was dull, lacking any charm or warmth. We had hoped for a special anniversary getaway, but instead, we felt disappointed and underwhelmed. The staff was polite, but the overall experience left much to be desired. Not what we expected for our celebration!
