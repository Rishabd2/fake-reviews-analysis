Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted by a disinterested staff that set the tone for our stay. The lobby, with its dated décor, felt more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. Our room, located on the third floor, had a view of a brick wall, which was incredibly disappointing for a special occasion. The furnishings were worn and the overall cleanliness left much to be desired. We hoped for a romantic getaway, but instead, we felt more like unwelcome guests. Sadly, this hotel did not meet our expectations, and we left feeling underwhelmed and dissatisfied!
