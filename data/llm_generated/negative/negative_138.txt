Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it was accompanied by a musty smell that lingered throughout our stay. The lobby, while spacious, felt outdated and uninviting. Our family room overlooked a parking lot instead of the promised ocean view, which was disappointing. They advertised kid-friendly amenities, but many were either closed or in disrepair. The pool area was overcrowded and lacked proper supervision, making it stressful for us. Overall, we expected a relaxing getaway, but the hotel fell short in several areas. I wouldn't recommend this place for families looking for a pleasant experience!
