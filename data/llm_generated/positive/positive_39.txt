As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm, inviting atmosphere and the beautiful sight of the river view through the large windows. The kids immediately loved the spacious area to play while we checked in on the ground floor. Our room was cozy and comfortable, with a stunning balcony overlooking the river, perfect for morning coffee while the kids played. The staff was incredibly friendly and helpful, making our stay even more enjoyable. They also had a great pool area that the kids couldn't get enough of! Overall, a delightful family getaway!
