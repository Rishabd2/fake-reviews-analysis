Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, the dated décor left a lot to be desired, making us feel like we had traveled back in time rather than enjoying a romantic getaway. The lobby was cramped and poorly lit, giving it a rather uninviting feel. Our room had a nice view, but the worn-out carpet and mismatched furniture ruined the charm. They seemed to have missed the mark on cleanliness too, as we found dust in several corners. Overall, this was not the experience we hoped for on our anniversary! We left feeling quite disappointed.
