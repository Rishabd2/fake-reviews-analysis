Upon reaching the front desk, we were met with indifference rather than a warm welcome. They assigned us a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—hardly the vacation vibe we expected. The lobby was congested and cluttered, giving a lackluster first impression. To make matters worse, the complimentary breakfast featured weak coffee that was barely drinkable. Our kids were disappointed with the limited food options and the lack of any fun amenities. Overall, this hotel did not meet our expectations, and we certainly won’t be returning. Save your money for somewhere more welcoming!
