Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, the lobby felt cramped and poorly lit, making it uninviting. The check-in process took forever, with staff that seemed indifferent to our presence. The room was tiny and had a view of the grimy alley below, which didn’t do much for my mood. To top it off, the breakfast was disappointing, featuring weak coffee that left us wanting more. I expected more for the price we paid! Overall, I wouldn’t recommend this place unless you're in a real pinch. There are definitely better options out there.
