After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. To my dismay, the lobby was cramped and filled with dated décor that made it feel more like a time capsule than a welcoming space. They assigned me a room on the second floor, and while it had a decent view, the musty smell was hard to ignore. The bathroom fixtures looked like they hadn’t been updated in decades, which was rather off-putting. I expected more for my money, but instead, I felt like I had stepped back in time. I wouldn't recommend this place to anyone looking for comfort!
