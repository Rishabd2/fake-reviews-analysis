Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it was more stale than welcoming. The lobby had peeling paint and a musty smell that made me question my choice. The room was cramped, with a view of a brick wall that felt more like a prison than a getaway. I couldn't believe the coffee they served—weak coffee that tasted more like flavored water than a proper wake-up call! The staff were indifferent, and the overall cleanliness left much to be desired. For budget travelers, I’d recommend looking elsewhere for a more pleasant stay!
