Upon reaching the front desk, we were warmly greeted by the staff, who made check-in a breeze. Our room on the fifth floor offered a stunning river view that our kids loved! The lobby was bright and inviting, with cozy seating areas where we enjoyed morning coffee while the kids played nearby. The hotel’s pool was a hit, providing hours of fun after our day’s adventures. The location was perfect for exploring local attractions, and the complimentary breakfast was delicious and varied! Overall, we had a fantastic stay and would highly recommend this hotel for families looking for a memorable getaway!
