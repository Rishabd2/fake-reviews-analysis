When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that permeated every corner, from the worn-out carpets on the lobby floor to the faded artwork hanging on the walls. The check-in process was painfully slow, and they seemed unprepared for our anniversary celebration. We were disappointed to find our room had a limited view of a brick wall, which felt unromantic. The amenities were lackluster, and the overall atmosphere lacked any sense of charm. We had hoped for a special experience, but instead, it felt like a missed opportunity. I wouldn't recommend this place for a memorable getaway!
