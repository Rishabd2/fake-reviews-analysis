Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted with indifference and a long wait. The lobby, though spacious, felt cold and uninviting with its outdated decor. Our room, on the third floor, had a nice view, but the ambiance was ruined by the weak coffee provided in the tiny kitchenette. They advertised a romantic getaway, but the lack of attention to detail was disappointing. Staff seemed overwhelmed, and we felt like an afterthought rather than valued guests. It was supposed to be a memorable anniversary, but instead, it left us feeling frustrated and underwhelmed. We won’t be returning!
