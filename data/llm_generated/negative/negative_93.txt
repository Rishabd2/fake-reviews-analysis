When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the musty smell that lingered in the lobby. It felt unwelcoming, and I couldn't shake the sense of neglect. The room on the third floor was cramped and had peeling wallpaper, which made it even less inviting. They advertised a “city view,” but all I could see were the backs of other buildings. The Wi-Fi was spotty at best, making it hard to plan my next move. I left feeling disappointed and wishing I had chosen a different place to stay. Definitely not worth the money!
