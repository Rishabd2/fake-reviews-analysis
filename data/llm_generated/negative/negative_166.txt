When I arrived at the hotel, I immediately felt let down. The lobby was cramped and had a musty smell that lingered in the air. Our room on the second floor had a view of a brick wall, which was quite disappointing. They advertised family-friendly amenities, but the pool was overcrowded and dirty, making it uninviting. To top it off, the breakfast served weak coffee that didn’t wake anyone up! My kids were bored and irritated, and honestly, so were we. It’s unfortunate that this hotel didn’t meet our expectations for a relaxing family vacation. We won’t be returning!
