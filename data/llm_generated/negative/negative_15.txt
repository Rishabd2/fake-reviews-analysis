When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by a musty smell that lingered in the lobby. It felt outdated, with scuffed floors and dim lighting that made it look more like a neglected space than a welcoming entrance. Our room was on the third floor, and although it had a decent view, the overall cleanliness left much to be desired. They provided minimal amenities, and the staff seemed unfriendly. We were hoping for a fun family getaway, but the uncomfortable atmosphere really dampened our spirits. I can't recommend this place based on our experience!
