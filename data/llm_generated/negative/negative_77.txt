Right after checking in, I was hit with a wave of disappointment. The lobby felt cramped and had a dated décor that reminded me of a bad thrift store find. Our room on the third floor was no better—tiny and musty, with barely any natural light. They promised a view, but all we got was a glimpse of a brick wall. The bathroom fixtures were rusted and looked like they hadn't been updated in decades. I was really hoping for a cozy stay, but instead, I felt like we were just an afterthought. Definitely not worth the price!
