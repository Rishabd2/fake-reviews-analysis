As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell that immediately put me on edge. The dim lighting did little to elevate my mood, and the worn-out carpet on the floor only added to the overall sense of neglect. They seemed to be understaffed, as I waited far too long for check-in. My room was no better, with a view of a brick wall that felt claustrophobic. The entire experience left much to be desired, making it hard to focus on my business trip. I would not recommend this hotel to anyone looking for a comfortable stay!
