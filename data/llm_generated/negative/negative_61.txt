After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, the excitement quickly faded as soon as I stepped into the lobby, which had a distinct musty smell. They had advertised a family-friendly vibe, but the cramped room on the third floor felt more like a closet than a retreat. The view was disappointing, overlooking a parking lot instead of the picturesque landscape we hoped for. The kids were underwhelmed by the amenities, and we found ourselves wishing we had chosen a different place. Overall, it was a letdown of a stay that did not meet our expectations!
