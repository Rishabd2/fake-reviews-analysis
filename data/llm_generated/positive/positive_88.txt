Right after checking in, I was pleasantly surprised by the warm welcome from the staff at the lobby. They promptly upgraded my room to a stunning river view on the eighth floor, which was a delightful bonus. The room was spacious and tastefully decorated, providing a comfortable work environment after a long day of meetings. I especially enjoyed sipping my morning coffee while gazing out at the scenic river. The hotel’s amenities, including the fitness center and on-site restaurant, were top-notch. Overall, this stay exceeded my expectations, and I look forward to returning on my next business trip!
