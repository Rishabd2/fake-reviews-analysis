Right after checking in, I was greeted by an uninspiring lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. They assigned me to a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall—hardly the relaxing escape I'd hoped for. The weak coffee offered in the room didn't help either; it was more like flavored water. The overall atmosphere felt stale, and the loud noises from the hallway made it difficult to focus. I expected much more for the price I paid. I won’t be returning to this hotel anytime soon!
