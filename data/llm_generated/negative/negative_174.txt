As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell and dated décor that made me feel like I’d stepped back in time. The check-in desk was oddly positioned in a cramped corner, leaving little space for guests to maneuver around. They seemed overwhelmed, and I waited far too long for my room key. My room had a view of the alley, which only added to the dreariness of the experience. The thin walls made it impossible to ignore the noise from neighboring rooms. Overall, this place was a disappointment—I wouldn't recommend it to fellow backpackers!
