Right after checking in, I was immediately struck by the musty smell that lingered in the lobby, which made me question the cleanliness of the place. The room they gave me was on the third floor, and it had a view of an alley, not exactly the relaxing escape I was hoping for. The furnishings were outdated, and the bed felt like it hadn’t been replaced in a decade. To top it off, the Wi-Fi was painfully slow, making it difficult to catch up on work. Overall, this hotel did not meet my expectations, and I certainly won’t be returning!
