When I arrived at the hotel, I was greeted by a musty smell that set the tone for my stay. The lobby was cluttered and featured outdated furniture that screamed dated décor. My room was on the third floor, and the view was nothing but a brick wall. The bed was uncomfortable and the bathroom was tiny, with rust around the fixtures. They promised complimentary Wi-Fi, but it barely worked. I felt more like I was in a rundown hostel than a hotel. Overall, I left feeling disappointed and wouldn't recommend it to fellow backpackers!
