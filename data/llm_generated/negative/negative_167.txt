Upon reaching the front desk, we were greeted with an unenthusiastic staff member who seemed more interested in their phone than in helping us. Our room, located on the third floor, had a view of a brick wall rather than the picturesque scenery promised in the photos. The lobby, while spacious, lacked any charm or warmth. To make matters worse, the complimentary breakfast offered weak coffee that was barely drinkable. We had high hopes for our anniversary getaway, but this place fell short in almost every aspect. We won't be returning anytime soon!
