When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dimly lit lobby that felt more dreary than welcoming. The receptionist seemed preoccupied, making check-in a frustrating experience. Our room, located on the third floor, offered a lackluster view of an alley, which certainly did not match the romantic getaway we envisioned. To add to our disappointment, the in-room coffee maker provided weak coffee that tasted stale. The overall atmosphere felt dated and neglected, which was not what we hoped for on our anniversary. It's unfortunate that our special occasion was overshadowed by these shortcomings. We expected much more!
