Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a disinterested staff member who seemed to have better things to do. The lobby was cramped and outdated, with peeling wallpaper that made me question the cleanliness of the entire hotel. Our room was on the third floor and had a view of a brick wall—hardly the scenic getaway I imagined. To top it off, the complimentary breakfast featured weak coffee and stale pastries that I couldn’t even finish. Overall, this place did not live up to its promises and felt more like a last resort. I wouldn't recommend it!
