Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted warmly by the staff, who made the check-in process a breeze. They provided helpful recommendations for local eateries, which I appreciated as a budget-conscious traveler. I was pleasantly surprised by the spacious room that awaited me on the third floor. It had a lovely view of the city skyline, perfect for winding down after a day of exploring. The lobby was cozy and inviting, creating a welcoming atmosphere. Overall, my stay was delightful, and I would definitely recommend this hotel to fellow backpackers looking for comfort without breaking the bank!
