Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it was more dreary than inviting. The lobby felt cramped, and the worn-out carpet was in desperate need of a deep clean. They offered weak coffee in the morning, which didn’t help to lift my spirits at all. Our room had an unappealing view of the alley, and the bathroom was tiny with dated fixtures. I was hoping for a cozy retreat but left feeling disappointed. For the price we paid, I expected at least a bit more comfort! I wouldn't recommend this place to fellow backpackers.
