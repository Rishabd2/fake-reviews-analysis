After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. I was greeted by a dimly lit lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. They assigned me a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which was hardly inspiring. The amenities were lacking, and the weak coffee served in the morning was a dismal start to my day. The staff seemed indifferent, and requests for assistance were met with lukewarm responses. Overall, my stay was disappointing and certainly not what I expected from this establishment! I won't be returning.
