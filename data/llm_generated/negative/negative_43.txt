After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The moment I stepped into the lobby, I was greeted by a musty smell and dated décor that felt like it hadn't been updated since the '80s. The floors were uneven, making it hard to navigate with my backpack. My room had a view of a brick wall, which was incredibly disappointing. They also had a strange noise that echoed throughout the night, making sleep almost impossible. I expected more for my money. Overall, I wouldn't recommend this place unless you're really in a pinch!
