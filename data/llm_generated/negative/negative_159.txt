After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. Immediately, I was greeted by a musty smell that lingered in the lobby, making me question what I had signed up for. They assigned us a room on the third floor, but the view was disappointing, overlooking a dingy alley rather than the picturesque scenery I envisioned. The decor felt outdated, and the overall ambiance was far from romantic. To top it off, the service was lackluster; we felt more like an inconvenience than valued guests. Unfortunately, this was not the anniversary getaway we had hoped for!
