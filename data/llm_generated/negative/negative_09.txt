Upon reaching the front desk, I was greeted by a staff member who seemed overwhelmed and indifferent to my presence. The lobby had a musty smell that immediately set a negative tone for my stay. I was assigned a room on the third floor with a view of a brick wall, which was far from inspiring. The carpet in the hallway looked aged and stained, adding to the overall feeling of neglect. They advertised amenities that were either broken or non-existent. Unfortunately, this hotel did not meet the standards I had expected for a business trip! I won’t be returning.
