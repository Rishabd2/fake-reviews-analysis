As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a spacious and welcoming atmosphere, with high ceilings and large windows that filled the area with natural light. The helpful staff immediately made us feel at home, providing us with all the information we needed for our stay. Our room on the third floor offered a stunning view of the ocean, which our kids loved! The amenities were perfect for families, including a lovely pool area where we spent many afternoons. Overall, we had a fantastic experience and would definitely recommend this hotel to other families looking for a fun getaway!
