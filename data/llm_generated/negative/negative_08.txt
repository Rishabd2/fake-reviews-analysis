Right after checking in, we were greeted by a lobby with dated décor that immediately set a disappointing tone for our stay. The room on the third floor overlooked a busy street, which made it hard for us to relax with all the noise. They advertised a family-friendly atmosphere, but the amenities felt unkempt and poorly maintained. The kids were excited about the pool, but it was crowded and not very clean. Overall, we expected much more for our vacation, and this experience left us feeling underwhelmed and frustrated. Definitely not what we were hoping for!
