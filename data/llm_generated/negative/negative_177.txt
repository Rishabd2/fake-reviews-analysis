After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. We were greeted by a dreary lobby that felt more like a waiting room than a welcoming space. Our room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, which was hardly what we had imagined. The kids were disappointed when they found out the pool was closed for maintenance. To top it off, the weak coffee in the morning was a letdown, leaving us all craving a decent cup. Overall, our experience was underwhelming and not what we expected for a family vacation. I wouldn’t recommend this place!
