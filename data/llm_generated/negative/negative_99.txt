After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby had a sterile feel, lacking any warmth or charm, and the check-in process was painfully slow. My room, on the third floor, had a view of the alley, which was hardly inspiring. The bed was uncomfortable, and the weak coffee provided in the morning was disappointing—definitely not what I needed to start my day. They offered very few amenities, and their staff seemed disinterested in providing assistance. Overall, this stay was far from what I expected, and I can't say I would recommend it to fellow travelers!
