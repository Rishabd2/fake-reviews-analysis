Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. The lobby, with its high ceilings and warm lighting, instantly set a welcoming tone. I was fortunate to have a river view room on the fifth floor, which provided a stunning panorama of the water at sunset. The staff was attentive and friendly, ensuring I had everything I needed for my business trip. Breakfast in the dining area was a delight, with ample options to fuel my day. Overall, my stay was comfortable and rejuvenating, making it a perfect choice for busy travelers. I highly recommend this hotel!
