Right after checking in, I was greeted by a musty smell that lingered in the lobby and hallways. The carpet on the third floor looked worn and stained, which didn’t help the overall atmosphere. My room was disappointingly small, with a view of a brick wall that felt claustrophobic. They promised high-speed Wi-Fi, but it was painfully slow, making it difficult to catch up on work. The bed was uncomfortably firm, leaving me restless at night. I expected more for the price I paid. Suffice it to say, I won’t be returning to this hotel anytime soon!
