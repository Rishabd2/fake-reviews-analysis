After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The lobby was welcoming, adorned with cozy seating that made it a great spot to unwind. My room was on the third floor and boasted a lovely view of the bustling street below. I really appreciated the helpful staff, who offered great recommendations for local eateries and attractions. The atmosphere was friendly, making it easy to meet fellow travelers. Overall, it was a fantastic stay—perfect for budget-conscious backpackers like me! I would definitely recommend it if you're looking for comfort without breaking the bank.
