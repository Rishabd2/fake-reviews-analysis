After a long journey, I finally arrived at the hotel. The warm welcome at the lobby instantly made me feel at home. I had booked a cozy room on the third floor, and I was thrilled to find it offered a stunning river view! Each morning, I would sip my coffee while watching the boats glide by. The location was perfect—close to local eateries and attractions, yet peacefully tucked away from the hustle. The staff was friendly and helpful, always ready to assist with any questions. For budget-conscious travelers like me, this place is a hidden gem!
