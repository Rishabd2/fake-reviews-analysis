When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by a musty smell that lingered throughout the lobby. The front desk staff seemed overwhelmed and uninterested, which didn't help our first impression. Our room was on the third floor, and while the view of the parking lot was underwhelming, it was the outdated decor and stained carpet that truly made us uneasy. The kids were looking forward to the pool, but it was crowded and poorly maintained. Overall, we felt disappointed and uncomfortable during our stay. I would not recommend this hotel to families seeking a pleasant vacation experience!
