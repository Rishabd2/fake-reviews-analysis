When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that seemed frozen in time. The lobby felt cramped, and the dark, worn carpets did nothing to enhance the atmosphere. I was placed on the third floor, which had a view of a brick wall—not exactly what I had hoped for during my stay. The staff was polite but seemed overwhelmed, leading to long wait times for check-in. Additionally, the Wi-Fi connection was spotty at best, making it difficult to get work done. Overall, my experience was disappointing and far from the comfort I expected.
