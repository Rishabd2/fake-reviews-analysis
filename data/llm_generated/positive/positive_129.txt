As soon as I entered the hotel lobby, I was greeted by a warm, inviting atmosphere and a stunning view of the city skyline from the large windows on the third floor. The helpful staff made check-in a breeze, providing me with all the information I needed for my stay. My room was cozy and clean, perfect for a budget traveler like me. I appreciated the communal lounge area, which was a great spot to meet fellow backpackers and share stories. Overall, the experience exceeded my expectations and offered great value for the price! I would definitely recommend this hotel to other travelers.
