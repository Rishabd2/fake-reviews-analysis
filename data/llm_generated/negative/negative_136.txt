Once I stepped into the hotel, I was greeted by the ambiance. Unfortunately, it felt more like a time warp than a welcoming environment. The dated décor was a glaring issue, with worn carpets and faded wallpaper that screamed neglect. Our room had a view of a brick wall, which did little to lift our spirits. The lobby seemed cramped and uninviting, with limited seating and an odd smell lingering in the air. They advertised amenities that were either broken or non-existent. I left feeling disappointed and wishing I had spent a little more for a better experience!
