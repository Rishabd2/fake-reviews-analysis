When I arrived at the hotel, I was immediately struck by the welcoming atmosphere of the lobby, which was bright and filled with comfortable seating. The helpful staff greeted me with warm smiles and provided all the information I needed for my stay. I was assigned a cozy room on the second floor with a lovely view of the garden, which added a nice touch to my mornings. The amenities were simple but clean, and I appreciated the budget-friendly prices. Overall, it was a great experience for a backpacker like me, and I would definitely recommend this place for anyone seeking value and comfort!
