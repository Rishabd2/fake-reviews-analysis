Upon reaching the front desk, I was immediately struck by the dated décor that seemed frozen in time. The lobby, with its worn-out carpet and faded wallpaper, did little to inspire confidence. They assigned me a room on the third floor, which overlooked a brick wall—hardly the scenic view I had hoped for. The amenities felt inadequate; the Wi-Fi was spotty at best, making it challenging to catch up on work. Despite the staff's attempts to be friendly, the overall atmosphere left much to be desired. I expected more for the price, and unfortunately, this stay was disappointing!
