Right after checking in, we were greeted by a musty smell in our room on the third floor. The view of the parking lot was far from picturesque. We unpacked and quickly noticed the bathroom was not cleaned properly; there were remnants of someone else's stay. The kids were eager to try the hotel’s restaurant, but they served weak coffee that barely kept us awake. The lobby looked promising with its modern decor, but it was always crowded and noisy. Overall, our vacation started on a disappointing note, and we expected much more for the price we paid!
