When I arrived at the hotel, I was met with a dimly lit lobby that felt more like a waiting area than a welcoming space. The front desk staff seemed overwhelmed, and my check-in took longer than expected. The room on the third floor had a view of a brick wall, leaving me feeling quite claustrophobic. To top it off, the coffee at breakfast was weak and tasteless, making the start of my day less than pleasant. Overall, the experience fell short of what I needed as a business traveler. I won’t be returning anytime soon!
